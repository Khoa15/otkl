### 1. Merging TEMPEST microwave and GOES-16 geostationary IR soundings...

*File: `Merging_TEMPEST_microwave_and_GOES_16_geostationary_IR_soundings_for_i__W4402862777.pdf`*

**Brief:** Combines CubeSat TEMPEST-D microwave data with GOES-16 ABI infrared data to improve water vapor retrievals.

**Main notes:**

- TEMPEST-D showed CubeSat radiometers can give stable microwave signals
- CubeSat radiometers lack companion sensors; ABI provides high temporal and spatial resolution
- Sensitivity tests show improved water vapor retrievals

### 2. The Asteroid Spectral Imager ASPECT on the Milani CubeSat

*File: `The_Asteroid_Spectral_Imager_ASPECT_on_the_Milani_CubeSat__W4414278273.pdf`*

**Brief:** Describes ASPECT, a hyperspectral camera on the Milani CubeSat to measure spectral properties of the Didymos asteroids.

**Main notes:**

- Near-infrared camera covering 650-1600 nm plus single-point spectrometer 1650-2500 nm
- Qualified to the Hera environmental specification
- Optical properties cross-calibrated with other Hera payloads
