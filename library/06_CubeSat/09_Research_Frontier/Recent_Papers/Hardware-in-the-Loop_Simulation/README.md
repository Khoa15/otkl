### 1. Addressing Inaccurate Phase Center Offsets in Precise Orbit Determination...

*File: `Addressing_Inaccurate_Phase_Center_Offsets_in_Precise_Orbit_Determinat__W4404535097.pdf`*

**Brief:** Examines errors in the offset between a satellite's centre of mass and its GNSS antenna phase centre in precise orbit determination, especially for agile satellites.

**Main notes:**

- Hardware-in-the-loop simulations show the offsets hurt agile satellites more
- Observability analysis and Monte Carlo simulations show rapid attitude changes allow direct estimation of the offsets
- Applying this gives a consistent, significant POD performance improvement
