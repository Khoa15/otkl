### 1. Research on design and control method of active vibration isolation system based on piezoelectric Stewart platform

*File: `Research_on_design_and_control_method_of_active_vibration_isolation_sy__W4406085054.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes an active vibration isolation system for space payloads using a piezoelectric-driven 6-DOF Stewart platform, since passive isolation is poor below 10 Hz.

**Main notes:**

- Passive systems fail on low-frequency vibration and resonances below 10 Hz
- Stewart platform characteristics are analysed and modelled
- Piezoelectric actuators drive the platform

### 2. The MAGnetometers for Innovation and Capability (MAGIC) Technology Demonstration on TRACERS

*File: `The_MAGnetometers_for_Innovation_and_Capability_MAGIC_Technology_Demon__W4412018057.pdf`*

**Brief:** Describes MAGIC, a technology demonstration on the NASA TRACERS SMEX mission flying new low-noise fluxgate magnetometer cores.

**Main notes:**

- Cores built at the University of Iowa
- Meant to replace dwindling supply of legacy Infinetics S1000 cores
- Flight-demonstrates the cores on TRACERS spacecraft
