### 2. Feasibility Analysis of a CubeSat Mission for Space Rider Observation

*File: `Feasibility_Analysis_of_a_CubeSat_Mission_for_Space_Rider_Observation__W4396568577.pdf`*

**Brief:** Motivated by growing space congestion and the need for in-orbit servicing, the paper studies the Space Rider Observer Cube (SROC), a payload deployed by the reusable Space Rider vehicle to closely inspect it.

**Main notes:**

- Close inspection is framed as a first step toward in-orbit servicing such as refuelling and repair
- SROC is deployed from Space Rider (an uncrewed reusable vehicle)
- Paper performs a feasibility analysis of the mission
