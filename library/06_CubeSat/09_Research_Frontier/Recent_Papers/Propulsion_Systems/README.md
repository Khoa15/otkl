### 1. Fast Reconfiguration Maneuvers of a Micro-satellite Constellation Based on ...

*File: `Fast_Reconfiguration_Maneuvers_of_a_Micro_satellite_Constellation_Base__W4392755156.pdf`*

**Brief:** Analyses formation flight of the RODiO CubeSat cluster with a small satellite in LEO to form a virtual SAR antenna.

**Main notes:**

- RODiO is an ASI-funded mission concept for distributed SAR on CubeSats with micro-propellers
- The small satellite transmits and RODiO receives
- Virtual antenna extension uses an along-track baseline

### 2. On-orbit Performance Evaluation of AQUARIUS: a Water Resistojet Propulsion System on 6U CubeSat EQUULEUS
