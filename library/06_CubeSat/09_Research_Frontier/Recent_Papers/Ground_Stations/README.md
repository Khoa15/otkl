### 1. Review of gridded climate products and their use in hydrological analyses

*File: `Review_of_gridded_climate_products_and_their_use_in_hydrological_analy__W4406248368.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reviews gridded climate datasets (precipitation, air temperature, humidity, wind speed, solar radiation) and criteria for selecting them as forcing data for hydrologic models.

**Main notes:**

- Climate forcing accuracy drives hydrologic model performance
- Compiles an overview of available gridded datasets
- Discusses considerations for dataset selection

### 2. Satellite-based entanglement distribution and quantum teleportation with continuous variables

*File: `Satellite_based_entanglement_distribution_and_quantum_teleportation_wi__W4394713736.pdf`*

**Brief:** Studies how atmospheric turbulence and other losses degrade continuous-variable entanglement distribution and teleportation between a ground station and a satellite.

**Main notes:**

- Error sources: diffraction, atmospheric attenuation, turbulence and detector inefficiency, in downlink and uplink
- An intermediate station (for state generation or beam refocusing) is added to improve fidelity
- Feasible for downlink up to LEO, and for uplink with the intermediate station
