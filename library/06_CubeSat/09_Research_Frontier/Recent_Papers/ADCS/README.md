### 1. Earth rotation-aware burn longitude guidance for targeted atmospheric entry interface of CubeSat

*File: `Earth_rotation_aware_burn_longitude_guidance_for_targeted_atmospheric__W7224791261.pdf`*

**Brief:** Studies end-of-life disposal of LEO CubeSats to limit space debris, proposing a way to choose the burn longitude of a single retrograde de-orbit manoeuvre so the CubeSat enters the atmosphere at a targeted longitude.

**Main notes:**

- Analytical guidance model accounts for Earth rotation during the coast phase, plus J2 and drag
- Integrated with GPS navigation and an EKF-assisted ADCS for autonomous burn execution
- Simulated for a 5.33 kg dry-mass 4U CubeSat at 500, 600 and 700 km altitude using GMAT

### 2. In-orbit demonstration of propulsive and drag-based formation control

*File: `In_orbit_demonstration_of_propulsive_and_drag_based_formation_control__W7133323440.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reports the first in-orbit demonstration of a hybrid formation-control strategy with two 2U CubeSats in the NanoFF mission, validating the TUBiX-5 platform for close-proximity operations.

**Main notes:**

- Thruster manoeuvres corrected an initial transverse drift of 45 km/day
- Differential drag then reduced relative distance, with passive collision safety via a helix orbit geometry
- Out-of-plane manoeuvres were executed in sunlit periods to refine cross-track separation
