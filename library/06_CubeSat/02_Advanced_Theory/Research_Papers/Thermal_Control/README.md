### 1. LncRNA NONRATT021972 siRNA regulates neuropathic pain behaviors in type 2 diabetic rats

*File: `LncRNA_NONRATT021972_siRNA_regulates_neuropathic_pain_behaviors_in_typ__W2340554437.pdf`*

**Brief:** Biomedical study on lncRNA siRNA treatment of diabetic neuropathic pain in rats.

**Main notes:**

- NONRATT021972 higher in DRG of diabetic rats; siRNA reduces it
- siRNA improved mechanical withdrawal threshold, thermal latency and nerve conduction velocity
- Lowered P2X7, GFAP and TNF-alpha expression
