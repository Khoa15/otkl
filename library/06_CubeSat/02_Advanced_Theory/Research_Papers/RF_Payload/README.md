### 1. CASSIOPE Enhanced Polar Outflow Probe (e-POP) Mission Overview

*File: `CASSIOPE_Enhanced_Polar_Outflow_Probe_e_POP_Mission_Overview__W2045075572.pdf`*

**Brief:** Overview of e-POP, a suite of 8 instruments on the CASSIOPE small satellite studying plasma outflow in the high-latitude ionosphere.

**Main notes:**

- Orbit 325 x 1500 km, 80 degrees inclination
- Instruments include plasma sensors, magnetometers, dual-frequency GPS receivers, CCD cameras, radio receiver and beacon
- Uses large storage and fast downlink of CASSIOPE's communications payload for 10 ms resolution

### 2. Introduction: The Cluster mission

*File: `IntroductionThe_Cluster_mission__W2000791690.pdf`*

**Brief:** Introduces ESA's Cluster mission, four satellites studying small-scale plasma structures in 3D.

**Main notes:**

- Launched in summer 2000 on two Soyuz rockets; tetrahedral constellation by end of August 2000
- 44 instruments commissioned; operational 1 February 2001
- Targets solar wind, bow shock, magnetopause, cusps, magnetotail and auroral zones
