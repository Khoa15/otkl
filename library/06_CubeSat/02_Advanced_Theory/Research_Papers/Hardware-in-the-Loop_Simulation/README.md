### 1. Characterization of multi-scale ionospheric irregularities using ground-based and space-based GNSS observations

*File: `Characterization_of_multi_scale_ionospheric_irregularities_using_groun__W3178697124.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of Virginia Tech studies on ionospheric irregularities and their effect on GNSS signals, using GNSS as an ionospheric remote-sensing tool.

**Main notes:**

- Covers background of irregularities and their GNSS impact
- Ground-based observations at high latitudes, passive and active, plus mid-latitude modelling
- Combines GNSS with radar, magnetometer and HF receivers

### 2. Hardware in the Loop Simulation for Model Predictive Control Applied to Satellite Attitude Control

*File: `Hardware_in_the_Loop_Simulation_for_Model_Predictive_Control_Applied_t__W2981965738.pdf`*

**Brief:** Develops a parameterized Model Predictive Control (MPC) for satellite attitude control and tests it with hardware-in-the-loop simulation.

**Main notes:**

- Attitude control must meet operating requirements and physical constraints
- Parameterized MPC is the proposed controller
- Validated using HIL simulation
