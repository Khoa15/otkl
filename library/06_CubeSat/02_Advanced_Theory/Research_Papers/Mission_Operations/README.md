### 1. The Flexible Microwave Payload-2: A SDR-Based GNSS-Reflectometer and L-Band Radiometer for CubeSats

*File: `The_Flexible_Microwave_Payload_2_A_SDR_Based_GNSS_Reflectometer_and_L__W3013724401.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents FMPL-2 on the FSSCat mission (winner of the 2017 ESA Small Sentinel Satellite Challenge), a passive microwave instrument combining a GNSS reflectometer and an L-band radiometer in 1U of a 6U CubeSat.

**Main notes:**

- Based on an FPGA virtualizing a dual-core ARM processor plus a software-defined radio
- Targets sea-ice coverage, sea-ice thickness and low-resolution soil moisture
- Paper covers design, tests in a controlled environment and calibration algorithms

### 2. Vectorized magnetometer for space applications using electrical readout of atomic scale defects in silicon carbide

*File: `Vectorized_magnetometer_for_space_applications_using_electrical_readou__W2559463100.pdf`*

**Brief:** Develops a self-calibrating solid-state magnetometer for small missions such as CubeSat swarms, measuring field-induced current changes in a SiC pn junction.

**Main notes:**

- Based on atomic-scale defects in silicon carbide
- No inductive sensing elements, high-frequency radio or optical circuitry needed
- Aimed at being more compact and lightweight than fluxgate and optically pumped designs
