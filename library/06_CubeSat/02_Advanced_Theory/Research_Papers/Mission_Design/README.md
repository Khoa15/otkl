### 1. Global monitoring of terrestrial chlorophyll fluorescence from moderate-spectral-resolution NIR satellite measurements

*File: `Global_monitoring_of_terrestrial_chlorophyll_fluorescence_from_moderat__W2126571464.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents a method to retrieve global terrestrial chlorophyll fluorescence from satellite near-infrared measurements, applied to GOME-2.

**Main notes:**

- Fluorescence indicates vegetation function, light-use efficiency and productivity
- Applications include carbon cycle modelling and agriculture
- Covers methodology, simulations and GOME-2 application

### 2. The GHGSat-D imaging spectrometer

*File: `The_GHGSat_D_imaging_spectrometer__W3088779370.pdf`*

**Brief:** Describes GHGSat-D ('Claire'), a demonstration satellite launched on 21 June 2016 to measure methane emissions at facility scale.

**Main notes:**

- Compact fixed-cavity Fabry-Perot imaging spectrometer in the shortwave infrared
- Effective spatial resolution 50 x 50 m over 12 x 12 km scenes
- Paper covers instrument design, forward model, retrieval and example emission retrievals
