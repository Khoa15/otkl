### 1. EARLINET: towards an advanced sustainable European aerosol lidar network

*File: `EARLINET_towards_an_advanced_sustainable_European_aerosol_lidar_networ__W2160282144.pdf`*

**Brief:** Describes the European Aerosol Research Lidar Network (EARLINET), founded in 2000 as a research project, and its evolution toward a sustainable advanced network.

**Main notes:**

- Network of ground-based aerosol lidar stations across Europe
- Founded in 2000
- Published in Atmospheric Measurement Techniques, 2014

### 2. Recent progress of seismic observation networks in Japan: Hi-net, F-net, K-NET and KiK-net

*File: `Recent_progress_of_seismic_observation_networks_in_Japan_Hi_net_F_net__W1985298050.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reports on Japan's dense seismic networks built after the 1995 Kobe earthquake.

**Main notes:**

- Hi-net: 696 high-sensitivity stations; F-net: 71 broadband stations
- KiK-net: 659 stations with uphole/downhole strong-motion pairs; K-NET: 1034 surface strong-motion stations
- Station numbers are as of April 2003
