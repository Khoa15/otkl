### 2. Nanobob: a CubeSat mission concept for quantum communication experiments

*File: `Nanobob_a_CubeSat_mission_concept_for_quantum_communication_experiment__W2767467024.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents a ground-to-space QKD mission concept and feasibility study for a nanosatellite payload.

**Main notes:**

- Entangled photon source on the ground; only detection in space
- Payload fits a 12U CubeSat (~12 dm3)
- Compatible with multiple QKD protocols
