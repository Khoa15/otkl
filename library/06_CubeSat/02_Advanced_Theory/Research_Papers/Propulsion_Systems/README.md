### 1. Deep-Space Optical Navigation for M-ARGO Mission

*File: `Deep_Space_Optical_Navigation_for_M_ARGO_Mission__W3205253336.pdf`*

**Brief:** Studies autonomous navigation for ESA's M-ARGO, a stand-alone deep-space CubeSat with electric propulsion headed to a near-Earth asteroid, to reduce operational costs.

**Main notes:**

- Uses line-of-sight directions to visible beacons in the Solar System
- Miniaturised optical sensors
- 3-sigma accuracy about 1000 km in position and 1 m/s in velocity in good conditions

### 2. Design and In-orbit Demonstration of REGULUS, an Iodine electric propulsion system

*File: `Design_and_In_orbit_Demonstration_of_REGULUS_an_Iodine_electric_propul__W3167916840.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents REGULUS, an iodine-fed electric propulsion system by T4i based on the Magnetically Enhanced Plasma Thruster, for CubeSats larger than 6U.

**Main notes:**

- Volume 1.5U, mass 2.5 kg including propellant
- Thrust 0.60 mN, Isp 600 s at 50 W, total impulse 3000 Ns
- Integrated on UniSat-7; in-orbit demonstration ongoing
