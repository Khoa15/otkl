### 2. Topographic modelling using UAVs compared with traditional survey meth...

*File: `Topographic_modelling_using_UAVs_compared_with_traditional_survey_meth__W2810909546.pdf`*

**Brief:** Compares UAV aerial photogrammetry surfaces with RTK GNSS and laser scanning for topographic control in a small open-pit quarry in southern Brazil, to assess the uncertainty of photogrammetric surfaces.

**Main notes:**

- Photogrammetry is cheaper and faster than traditional methods
- UAV surfaces were consistent with the references, with a few outliers from vegetation, water and moving objects
- Reference methods were RTK GNSS and laser scanning
