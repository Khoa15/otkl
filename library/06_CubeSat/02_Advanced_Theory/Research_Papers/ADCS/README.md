### 1. CubeSat quantum communications mission

*File: `CubeSat_quantum_communications_mission__W2606282679.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes a CubeSat deployed from the ISS to perform orbit-to-ground transmission of entanglement and quantum key distribution (QKD). The paper argues that small satellites can cut the cost and risk of raising quantum technologies to space readiness.

**Main notes:**

- Targets orbit-to-ground entanglement transmission and QKD from a CubeSat
- Relies on nanosatellite ADCS, miniaturised target acquisition and tracking sensors
- Small-satellite route is presented as a way to leap-frog conventional space development

### 2. Gyroless Spin-Stabilization Controller and Deorbiting Algorithm for CubeSats

*File: `Gyroless_Spin_Stabilization_Controller_and_Deorbiting_Algorithm_for_Cu__W3048820250.pdf`*

**Brief:** Addresses active deorbiting of CubeSats, which usually lack attitude determination and propulsion, to reduce space debris. It presents a simple algorithm using electric propulsion with minimal sensing and actuation.

**Main notes:**

- Algorithm has two stages: spin-stabilization control and deorbiting-phase detection
- Spin-stabilization is inspired by the B-dot controller and needs no gyroscopes
- Designed for the tight constraints of nanosatellites
