### 1. Biomaterials technology and policies in the building sector: a review

*File: `Biomaterials_technology_and_policies_in_the_building_sector_a_review__W4391320605.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of renewable biomaterials as alternatives to traditional building materials, with policies and life cycle assessment case studies.

**Main notes:**

- Bio-based materials could reduce over 320,000 tons of CO2 emissions by 2050
- Reported benefits include 40% lower water absorption, 8.7% lower energy use and 6.7% better acoustic absorption
- Traditional materials still dominate; policymaker understanding is limited
