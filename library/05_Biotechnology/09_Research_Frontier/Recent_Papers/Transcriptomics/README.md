### 1. SQANTI3: curation of long-read transcriptomes for accurate identification of known and novel isoforms

*File: `SQANTI3_curation_of_long_read_transcriptomes_for_accurate_identificati__W4393004003.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents SQANTI3, a tool for quality control, curation and annotation of long-read transcript models.

**Main notes:**

- Computes quality descriptors of transcript models, junctions and ends
- Identifies artifacts and replaces them with reliable sequences
- Includes functional annotation for iso-transcriptomics
- Works with PacBio and Oxford Nanopore data
