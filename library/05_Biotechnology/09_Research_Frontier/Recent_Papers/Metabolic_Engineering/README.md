### 1. Adeno-associated virus as a delivery vector for gene therapy of human diseases

*File: `Adeno_associated_virus_as_a_delivery_vector_for_gene_therapy_of_human__W4393581315.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of AAV biology, vector engineering, manufacturing and clinical use in gene therapy.

**Main notes:**

- Seven rAAV products have regulatory approval
- Concerns: immune responses, genotoxicity, hepatotoxicity, thrombotic microangiopathy, neurotoxicity
- Covers trials for ocular, neurological, metabolic, hematological, neuromuscular, cardiovascular diseases and cancers
- Manufacturing demand poses challenges

### 2. FOXO1 enhances CAR T cell stemness, metabolic fitness and efficacy

*File: `FOXO1_enhances_CAR_T_cell_stemness_metabolic_fitness_and_efficacy__W4394694498.pdf`*

**Brief:** Studies why CAR T therapy works poorly in solid tumours and shows FOXO1 can improve CAR T cell persistence and fitness.

**Main notes:**

- Solid-tumour limits come from immunosuppressive microenvironment and dysfunctional T cells
- Positive clinical outcomes linked to a more stem-like T cell state
- Published in Nature, May 2024
- Extracted text ends before the detailed results
