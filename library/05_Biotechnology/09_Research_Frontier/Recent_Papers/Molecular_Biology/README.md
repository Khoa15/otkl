### 1. Sequence modeling and design from molecular to genome scale with Evo

*File: `Sequence_modeling_and_design_from_molecular_to_genome_scale_with_Evo__W4404349982.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents Evo, a genomic foundation model for prediction and generation across DNA, RNA and protein.

**Main notes:**

- 7 billion parameters, 131 kb context at single-nucleotide resolution
- Trained on 2.7M prokaryotic and phage genomes
- Zero-shot function prediction competitive with or better than domain-specific language models

### 2. Vascular endothelial growth factor signaling in health and disease: from molecular mechanisms to therapeutic perspective

*File: `Vascular_endothelial_growth_factor_signaling_in_health_and_disease_fro__W4410473472.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of VEGF signaling in vasculogenesis, angiogenesis and lymphangiogenesis, and its disease roles.

**Main notes:**

- Excess VEGF drives tumor growth and metastasis; deficiency impairs wound healing and ischemic repair
- Anti-VEGF antibodies and kinase inhibitors used in oncology and ophthalmology
- Challenges: resistance, suboptimal efficacy, adverse effects
