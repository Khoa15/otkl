### 1. Genomic data in the All of Us Research Program

*File: `Genomic_data_in_the_All_of_Us_Research_Program__W4391925485.pdf`*

**Brief:** Describes the genomics data release of the All of Us Research Program, a diverse US cohort aiming to enrol at least one million people.

**Main notes:**

- 245,388 clinical-grade genome sequences; 77% of participants from under-represented communities
- Over 1 billion variants found, over 275 million previously unreported
- 3,724 variants tested for 117 diseases with high replication across European and African ancestry
- Summary data public; individual data via researcher access

### 2. edgeR 4.0: powerful differential analysis of sequencing data with expanded functionality and improved support for small

*File: `edgeR_v4_powerful_differential_analysis_of_sequencing_data_with_expand__W4406778273.pdf`*

**Brief:** Announces edgeR version 4, an R/Bioconductor package for differential analysis of sequencing count data.

**Main notes:**

- edgeR pioneered negative binomial models and generalized linear models for counts
- Uses empirical Bayes moderation for small replicate numbers
- Version 4 improves support for small counts and larger datasets
