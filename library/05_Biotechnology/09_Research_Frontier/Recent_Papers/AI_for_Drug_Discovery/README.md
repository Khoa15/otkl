### 1. DynamicBind: predicting ligand-specific protein-ligand complex structure with a deep equivariant generative model

*File: `DynamicBind_predicting_ligand_specific_protein_ligand_complex_structur__W4391542588.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents DynamicBind, a deep learning method that models protein flexibility upon ligand binding, unlike rigid docking.

**Main notes:**

- Uses equivariant geometric diffusion networks to build a smooth energy landscape
- Recovers ligand-specific conformations from unbound structures without holo-structures
- State-of-the-art on docking and virtual screening benchmarks
