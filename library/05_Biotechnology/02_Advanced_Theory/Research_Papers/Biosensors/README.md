### 1. Current hydrogel advances in physicochemical and biological response-driven biomedical application diversity

*File: `Current_hydrogel_advances_in_physicochemical_and_biological_response_d__W4200064246.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of how hydrogel properties (stiffness, pore size, viscoelasticity, degradability, ligand presentation) influence cell behavior and support biomedical use.

**Main notes:**

- Introduces hydrogel properties and fabrication approaches
- Discusses effects on integrin clustering, focal adhesions, cytoskeleton rearrangement and YAP signaling
- Aims at lab-to-clinic translation of hydrogel therapies

### 2. Rapid methods for the detection of foodborne bacterial pathogens: principles, applications, advantages and limitations

*File: `Rapid_methods_for_the_detection_of_foodborne_bacterial_pathogens_princ__W2151996078.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of rapid methods for detecting foodborne bacterial pathogens, motivated by the rising incidence of foodborne disease as a public health problem.

**Main notes:**

- Describes principles and applications of rapid detection methods
- Discusses advantages and limitations of each approach
- Published in Frontiers in Microbiology, 2015
