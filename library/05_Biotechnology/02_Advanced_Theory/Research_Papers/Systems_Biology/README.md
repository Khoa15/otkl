### 1. DNA methylation age of human tissues and cell types

*File: `DNA_methylation_age_of_human_tissues_and_cell_types__W1974047233.pdf`*

**Brief:** Builds a multi-tissue predictor of age from DNA methylation to test whether it is accurate across tissues and biologically meaningful.

**Main notes:**

- Developed using 8,000 samples from 82 datasets covering 51 tissues and cell types
- Age is near zero for embryonic and induced pluripotent stem cells and correlates with passage number
- Age acceleration is highly heritable; applies to chimpanzee tissues
- 20 cancer types in 6,000 samples showed significant age acceleration, on average 36 years
