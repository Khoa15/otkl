*File: `Development_of_therapeutic_antibodies_for_the_treatment_of_diseases__W2999958881.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of therapeutic monoclonal antibody development, summarizing the evolution of antibody engineering since the first FDA approval in 1986.

**Main notes:**

- Antibodies are the top-selling drug class; market valued at about US$115.2 billion in 2018
- Used against cancers, autoimmune, metabolic and infectious diseases

### 2. Flavonoids: biosynthesis, biological functions, and biotechnological applications

*File: `Flavonoids_biosynthesis_biological_functions_and_biotechnological_appl__W1966542159.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of flavonoid secondary metabolites in plants, covering biosynthesis, regulation and metabolic engineering uses.

**Main notes:**

- Biosynthesis regulated by MYB, bHLH and WD40 transcription factors
- Functions include defense against UV-B and pathogens, nodulation and pollen fertility
- Discusses genetic engineering strategies and their achievements
