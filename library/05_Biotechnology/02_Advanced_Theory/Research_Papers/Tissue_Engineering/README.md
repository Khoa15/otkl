### 1. Bone regeneration: current concepts and future directions

*File: `Bone_regeneration_current_concepts_and_future_directions__W2004337957.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of bone regeneration biology and strategies to augment impaired or insufficient bone repair.

**Main notes:**

- Clinical needs include large defects from trauma, infection, tumour resection, non-unions, osteoporosis
- Strategies: autologous graft (gold standard), vascularised fibula graft, allograft, growth factors, scaffolds, osteoprogenitor cells, distraction osteogenesis
- Tissue engineering and gene therapy are under investigation

### 2. Recent advances in 3D printing of biomaterials

*File: `Recent_advances_in_3D_printing_of_biomaterials__W2172143926.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of major materials and technology advances in 3D printing for biomedical devices, implants, scaffolds and drug delivery over the last five years.

**Main notes:**

- Covers 3D Printing, FDM, selective laser sintering, stereolithography and bioprinting
- Highlights examples in tissue engineering
- Identifies limitations that must be solved before complex tissues and organs can be regenerated
