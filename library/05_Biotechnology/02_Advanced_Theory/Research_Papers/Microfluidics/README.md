### 2. Simple Surface Modification of Poly(dimethylsiloxane) via Surface Segregating Smart Polymers for Biomicrofluidics

*File: `Simple_Surface_Modification_of_Poly_dimethylsiloxane_via_Surface_Segre__W2946593644.pdf`*

**Brief:** Addresses the hydrophobicity of PDMS, which causes non-specific protein adsorption in microfluidic devices. Proposes blending PDMS-PEG copolymers into PDMS so they segregate to the surface.

**Main notes:**

- No post-cure surface treatment needed
- Contact angles as low as 23.6 degrees +/- 1
- Hydrophilicity retained for at least twenty months
- Keeps cell biocompatibility, transparency and mechanical properties
