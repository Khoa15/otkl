### 1. Applications of genome editing technology in the targeted therapy of human diseases: mechanisms, advances and prospects

*File: `Applications_of_genome_editing_technology_in_the_targeted_therapy_of_h__W2998881619.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of three genome editing technologies (ZFNs, TALENs, CRISPR/Cas9) and their use in treating human diseases.

**Main notes:**

- Programmable nucleases moved gene editing from concept toward clinical practice
- Focus on eukaryotic cells and animal models
- Covers derivative reagents as editing tools and prospective therapies

### 2. The CRISPR tool kit for genome editing and beyond

*File: `The_CRISPR_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and_beyond__W2803581084.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of CRISPR-based tools, arguing that Cas9 is now more than a gene-editing tool through its catalytically inactive forms.

**Main notes:**

- Gives a brief history of gene-editing tools
- Inactive Cas9 applications include gene regulation, epigenetic editing, chromatin engineering and imaging
- Concludes with future directions and broader impact
