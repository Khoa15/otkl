### 1. Engineering microbial consortia by division of labor

*File: `Engineering_microbial_consortia_by_division_of_labor__W2913553075.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of dividing a metabolic pathway among several microbial strains to relieve metabolic burden in bioprocesses.

**Main notes:**

- Natural and synthetic consortia can avoid performance drops from over-burdened cells
- Examples include consolidated bioprocessing and cross-feeding such as vitamin C fermentation
- Challenges: subpopulation dynamics, cheaters, intermediate dilution, transport barriers
- Stable consortia need optimized strain inoculation and cultivation

### 2. Operational strategies, monitoring and control of heterologous protein production in Pichia pastoris

*File: `Operational_strategies_monitoring_and_control_of_heterologous_protein__W1595540384.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of how Pichia pastoris is used for heterologous protein production under different promoters. It covers strategies to maximize yield, productivity and batch-to-batch reproducibility.

**Main notes:**

- Covers PAOX promoter phenotypes and alternative promoters such as GAP
- Discusses medium composition and growth kinetics
- Compares operation modes from fed-batch to continuous culture
- Covers monitoring and control for robust industrial processes
