### 1. CogACT: A Foundational Vision-Language-Action Model for Synergizing Cognition and Action in Robotic Manipulation

*File: `CogACT_A_Foundational_Vision_Language_Action_Model_for_Synergizing_Cog__W4405031329.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents CogACT, a vision-language-action model for robotic manipulation that separates cognition from action. It reports success rates against action module size.

**Main notes:**

- Compares average success rate with the size of the action module (small, base, large)
- Shows examples such as picking up an unseen hammer
- From Tsinghua University and Microsoft Research Asia

### 2. Cosmos Policy: Fine-Tuning Video Models for Visuomotor Control and Planning

*File: `Cosmos_Policy_Fine_Tuning_Video_Models_for_Visuomotor_Control_and_Plan__W7125496427.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents Cosmos Policy, a robot policy fine-tuned from the NVIDIA Cosmos-Predict2-2B video foundation model for visuomotor control and planning.

**Main notes:**

- Fine-tuned from the Cosmos-Predict2-2B video foundation model
- Handles multimodal inputs and multi-view camera images
- Predicts a robot action chunk among other outputs
