### 1. Grasp Multiple Objects with One Hand

*File: `Grasp_Multiple_Objects_With_One_Hand__W4392502288.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents MultiGrasp, a method for grasping multiple objects simultaneously with a dexterous hand. It is inspired by the kinematics of the human hand.

**Main notes:**

- Demonstrated with the Shadow Hand grasping several objects at once
- Relevant to object transfer and in-hand manipulation
- Published in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (2024)

### 2. Understanding URDF: A Dataset and Analysis

*File: `Understanding_URDF_A_Dataset_and_Analysis__W4393140159.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents what the authors say is the first dataset of URDF robot description files, with metadata on robot type, manufacturer and source. URDF is the common format for robot kinematics and dynamics models.

**Main notes:**

- URDF files come from industrial and research organizations
- Metadata describes each robot, its type, manufacturer and source
- Motivated by simulating increasingly complex robots before deployment
