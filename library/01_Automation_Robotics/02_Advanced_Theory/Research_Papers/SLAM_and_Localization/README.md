### 2. Visual SLAM algorithms: a survey from 2010 to 2016

*File: `Visual_SLAM_algorithms_a_survey_from_2010_to_2016__W2621274416.pdf`*

**Brief:** A survey of visual SLAM algorithms from 2010 to 2016. SLAM estimates sensor motion and reconstructs structure in an unknown environment, and visual SLAM does this with cameras.

**Main notes:**

- SLAM means simultaneous localization and mapping
- Visual SLAM (vSLAM) is based on visual information from cameras
- Published in IPSJ Transactions on Computer Vision and Applications (2017)
