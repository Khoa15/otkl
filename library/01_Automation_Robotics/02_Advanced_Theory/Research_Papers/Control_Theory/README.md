### 1. Control of MXenes' electronic properties through termination and intercalation

*File: `Control_of_MXenes_electronic_properties_through_termination_and_interc__W2912706548.pdf`*

**Brief:** A materials-science paper on tuning the electronic conductivity of MXenes, a family of highly conductive 2D materials. It targets higher conductivity by tailoring surface chemistry, since theory predicts surface terminations affect the Fermi-level density of states.

**Main notes:**

- MXenes are noted for EMI shielding, chemical sensing and energy storage
- Density functional theory predicts terminations strongly influence the Fermi-level density of states
- Goal is to raise electronic conductivity through termination and intercalation control
- Not about control theory despite its folder

### 2. Deep learning for universal linear embeddings of nonlinear dynamics

*File: `Deep_learning_for_universal_linear_embeddings_of_nonlinear_dynamics__W2777417212.pdf`*

**Brief:** Uses deep learning to find coordinates in which nonlinear dynamics become approximately linear, based on Koopman operator eigenfunctions. This would let linear theory be used for prediction, estimation and control.

**Main notes:**

- Koopman eigenfunctions give intrinsic coordinates that globally linearize dynamics but are hard to identify
- The network learns Koopman eigenfunction representations from data
- Network is parsimonious and interpretable by construction, embedding dynamics on a low-dimensional manifold

### 4. Viable supply chain model: integrating agility, resilience and sustainability perspectives

*File: `Viable_supply_chain_model_integrating_agility_resilience_and_sustainab__W3026347338.pdf`*

**Brief:** Introduces the viable supply chain (VSC) concept, the ability of a supply chain to survive in a changing environment through redesign and replanning. It draws lessons from the COVID-19 pandemic.

**Main notes:**

- It spans three perspectives: agility, resilience and sustainability
- Published in Annals of Operations Research (2022)
