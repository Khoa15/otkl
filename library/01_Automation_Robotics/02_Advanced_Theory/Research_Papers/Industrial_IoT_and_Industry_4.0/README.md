*File: `Variational_LSTM_Enhanced_Anomaly_Detection_for_Industrial_Big_Data__W3086419524.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes an anomaly detection method for high-dimensional industrial big data in the Industrial IoT. It targets the inconsistency between dimensionality reduction and feature retention.

- Published in IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics (2021)
