*File: `AI_Based_Modeling_Techniques_Applications_and_Research_Issues_Towards__W4225716729.pdf`*

**Main notes:**

- Frames AI as a leading technology of the Fourth Industrial Revolution
- Covers several types of AI, such as analytical and functional
- Published in SN Computer Science (2022)

### 2. Blockchain Platform for Industrial Internet of Things

*File: `Blockchain_Platform_for_Industrial_Internet_of_Things__W2540162589.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes a blockchain platform for industrial IoT and cloud-based manufacturing. It addresses the need for a trusted intermediary in on-demand manufacturing.

**Main notes:**

- IIoT is used for manufacturing automation, remote diagnostics, prognostic health management and supply chains
- Cloud-based manufacturing gives on-demand access to manufacturing resources
- A trusted intermediary is needed, which the blockchain platform is meant to supply
