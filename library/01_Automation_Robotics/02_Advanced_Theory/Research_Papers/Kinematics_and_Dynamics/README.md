### 1. Finite-Time Convergence Adaptive Fuzzy Control for Dual-Arm Robot With Unknown Kinematics and Dynamics

*File: `Finite_Time_Convergence_Adaptive_Fuzzy_Control_for_Dual_Arm_Robot_With__W2887685036.pdf`*

**Brief:** Develops an adaptive fuzzy controller for a dual-arm robot with uncertain kinematics and dynamics and internal forces from grasped objects.

**Main notes:**

- An approximate Jacobian matrix handles uncertain kinematic control
- A decentralized fuzzy logic controller handles the dynamics
- Targets finite-time convergence
- Published in IEEE Transactions on Fuzzy Systems (2019)
