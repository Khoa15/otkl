### 1. From the Desks of ROS Maintainers: A Survey of Modern & Capable Mobile Robotics Algorithms in ROS 2

*File: `From_the_desks_of_ROS_maintainers_A_survey_of_modern_capable_mobile_ro__W4385325374.pdf`*

**Brief:** A survey by ROS 2 maintainers of modern, capable mobile robotics algorithms available in ROS 2, which targets product-grade robotic systems.

**Main notes:**

- ROS 2 is used in space missions, agriculture equipment and multi-robot fleets
- Its design targets product-grade and modern robotic systems
- Written by ROS 2 project lead and maintainers

### 2. SLAM Toolbox: SLAM for the dynamic world

*File: `SLAM_Toolbox_SLAM_for_the_dynamic_world__W3163573497.pdf`*

- Target environments are dynamic and often very large
- Published in the Journal of Open Source Software (2021)
