### 1. Human-Centered Interfaces in Industrial Control Systems: A Review of Usability and Visual Feedback

*File: `HUMAN_CENTERED_INTERFACES_IN_INDUSTRIAL_CONTROL_SYSTEMS_A_REVIEW_OF_US__W7104545578.pdf`*

**Brief:** Investigates how human-centered interface attributes, mainly usability and visual feedback quality, affect operator cognition and performance in industrial control systems such as SCADA, DCS and PLC environments.

**Main notes:**

- Draws on HCI, situation awareness and mental workload theory
- Usability is defined as learnability, efficiency, error tolerance and consistency
- Visual feedback quality covers clarity, salience and coding
