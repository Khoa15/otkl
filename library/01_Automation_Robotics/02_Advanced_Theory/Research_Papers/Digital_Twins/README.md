### 1. Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform for Physical AI

*File: `Cosmos_World_Foundation_Model_Platform_for_Physical_AI__W4406191642.pdf`*

**Brief:** NVIDIA presents the Cosmos platform for building customized world models for Physical AI. It frames a world foundation model as a general-purpose world model that can be fine-tuned for downstream applications.

**Main notes:**

- Argues Physical AI needs a digital twin of itself (policy model) and of the world (world model)
- Platform covers a video curation pipeline, pre-trained world foundation models, post-training examples and video tokenizers
- Made open for Physical AI builders

### 4. Digital twins for health: a scoping review

*File: `Digital_twins_for_health_a_scoping_review__W4393094536.pdf`*

**Brief:** A scoping review of digital twins for health (DT4H), noting a recent surge of use in healthcare. It considers their promise for care management, disease treatment and prevention, and well-being.

**Main notes:**

- Describes DT4H as promising to revolutionize healthcare management and delivery
- Use of digital twins has proliferated across fields and industries
- Published in npj Digital Medicine
