## Chapter summaries

Each chapter (PDF) in this topic, summarized from its first two pages.

### 1. A Survey on Offline Reinforcement Learning: Taxonomy, Review, and Open Problems

*File: `A_Survey_on_Offline_Reinforcement_Learning_Taxonomy_Review_and_Open_Pr__W4360584316.pdf`*

**Brief:** A survey of offline RL, which learns only from static datasets and so suits settings where environment interaction is costly or dangerous. It offers a taxonomy and open problems.

**Main notes:**

- Offline RL learns exclusively from previously collected interactions
- Motivated by the high cost and danger of online interaction
- Provides taxonomy, review and open problems

### 2. Champion-level drone racing using deep reinforcement learning

*File: `Champion_level_drone_racing_using_deep_reinforcement_learning__W4386285856.pdf`*

**Brief:** Introduces Swift, an autonomous system that races physical drones at the level of human champions in first-person-view racing, estimating state only from onboard sensors.

**Main notes:**

- Challenge is flying at physical limits using only onboard sensing
- Swift is based on deep reinforcement learning
- Published in Nature (2023)

### 3. Voronoi-Based Multi-Robot Autonomous Exploration in Unknown Environments via Deep Reinforcement Learning

*File: `Voronoi_Based_Multi_Robot_Autonomous_Exploration_in_Unknown_Environmen__W3095315965.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents a multi-robot exploration method for unknown environments using Voronoi-based techniques and deep reinforcement learning.

**Main notes:**

- Combines Voronoi partitioning with deep RL
- Targets multi-robot exploration of unknown environments
- Published in IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology (2020)

### 4. What is intrinsic motivation? A typology of computational approaches

*File: `What_is_intrinsic_motivation_A_typology_of_computational_approaches__W2000514530.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes a typology of computational approaches to intrinsic motivation, which drives spontaneous exploration and curiosity and is central to open-ended cognitive development.

**Main notes:**

- Intrinsic motivation is a key concept in developmental psychology
- Argued to be crucial for open-ended cognitive development
- Published in Frontiers in Neurorobotics (2007)
