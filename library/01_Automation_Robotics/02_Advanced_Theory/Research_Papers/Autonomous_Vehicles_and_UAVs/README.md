### 1. A Survey on the Convergence of Edge Computing and AI for UAVs: Opportunities and Challenges

*File: `A_Survey_on_the_Convergence_of_Edge_Computing_and_AI_for_UAVs_Opportun__W4285105424.pdf`*

**Brief:** A survey of how edge computing and AI can be combined for UAV (drone) based IoT applications. It addresses the difficulty of cloud-based AI meeting the low-latency and low-energy needs of UAV tasks such as computer vision and path planning.

**Main notes:**

- Notes that 5G networks have enabled many UAV-based IoT applications
- Says UAV success depends heavily on AI such as computer vision and path planning
- Argues cloud-based AI struggles to meet strict latency and energy requirements
- Reviews opportunities and challenges of moving AI to the edge

### 2. Last-mile delivery concepts: a survey from an operational research perspective

*File: `Last_mile_delivery_concepts_a_survey_from_an_operational_research_pers__W3087935424.pdf`*

**Brief:** A survey of last-mile parcel delivery concepts viewed through operational research. It responds to growing e-commerce parcel volumes that increase vans, congestion, pollution and health impacts in urban areas.

**Main notes:**

- Motivated by e-commerce growth and urban congestion from delivery vans
- Surveys the many novel delivery concepts proposed in recent years
- Takes an operational research perspective on planning these concepts
