## Chapter summaries

Each chapter (PDF) in this topic, summarized from its first two pages.

### 1. Multi-Objective Loss Balancing for Physics-Informed Deep Learning

*File: `Multi_Objective_Loss_Balancing_for_Physics_Informed_Deep_Learning__W4408489371.pdf`*

**Brief:** Studies how weighting the competing loss terms of PINNs affects training, and proposes a new self-adaptive scheme.

**Main notes:**

- Reviews Learning Rate Annealing, GradNorm and SoftAdapt.
- Proposes ReLoBRaLo (Relative Loss Balancing with Random Lookback).
- Evaluated on Burgers', Kirchhoff plate bending and Helmholtz equations, forward and inverse.

### 2. Tackling the Curse of Dimensionality with Physics-Informed Neural Networks

*File: `Tackling_the_curse_of_dimensionality_with_physics_informed_neural_netw__W4396690897.pdf`*

**Brief:** Addresses the high cost of solving high-dimensional PDEs by scaling up PINNs.

**Main notes:**

- Introduces Stochastic Dimension Gradient Descent (SDGD), which samples subsets of per-dimension gradient pieces each iteration.
- Convergence and other properties are proved theoretically.
- Solves HJB and Schrodinger equations in tens of thousands of dimensions on a single GPU.
