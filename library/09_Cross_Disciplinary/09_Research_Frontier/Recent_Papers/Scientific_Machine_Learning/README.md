### 1. Structured information extraction from scientific text with large language models

*File: `Structured_information_extraction_from_scientific_text_with_large_lang__W4391836235.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents a simple approach to joint named entity recognition and relation extraction by fine-tuning LLMs such as GPT-3 and Llama-2 on scientific text.

**Main notes:**

- Tested on dopant-host linking, metal-organic framework cataloging and general materials information extraction.
- Works on single sentences or whole paragraphs.
- Output can be English sentences or structured JSON objects.

### 2. The AI Scientist: Towards Fully Automated Open-Ended Scientific Discovery

*File: `The_AI_Scientist_Towards_Fully_Automated_Open_Ended_Scientific_Discove__W4402952666.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents a framework in which frontier LLMs conduct research end to end and communicate findings.

**Main notes:**

- Generates ideas, writes code, runs experiments and visualizes results.
- Writes a full paper and runs a simulated review process.
- Process can repeat to build an archive of knowledge.
