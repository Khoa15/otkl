### 1. LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems for Software Engineering: Literature Review, Vision and the Road Ahead

*File: `LLM_Based_Multi_Agent_Systems_for_Software_Engineering_Literature_Revi__W4406325768.pdf`*

**Brief:** A systematic review of LLM-based multi-agent systems applied across the software development lifecycle, with a research agenda.

**Main notes:**

- Maps current LMA applications across SDLC stages.
- Two case studies demonstrate capabilities and limitations of state-of-the-art frameworks.
- Identifies research gaps and proposes an agenda on individual agent capability and agent synergy.

### 2. Memory in the Age of AI Agents: A Survey - Forms, Functions and Dynamics

*File: `Memory_in_the_Age_of_AI_Agents__W4417459816.pdf`*

**Brief:** A survey of memory in foundation-model-based agents, a core capability for long-horizon reasoning, continual adaptation and interaction with complex environments.

**Main notes:**

- Organizes agent memory by forms, functions and dynamics.
- Notes the field's rapid growth and increasing fragmentation.
- Written by a large multi-institution author team.
