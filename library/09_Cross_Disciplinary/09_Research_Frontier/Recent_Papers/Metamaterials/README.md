### 1. Multi-Physical Lattice Metamaterials Enabled by Additive Manufacturing

*File: `Multi_Physical_Lattice_Metamaterials_Enabled_by_Additive_Manufacturing__W4406629728.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of lattice metamaterials made by additive manufacturing, focused on multi-physical properties, interaction mechanisms and multifunctional applications, which earlier reviews treated mostly for single physical fields.

**Main notes:**

- Categorizes lattices as homogeneous, inhomogeneous and other forms, with design principles and AM processes.
- Discusses benefits and drawbacks of different AM techniques.
- Covers structure-mechanism-property relationships and interaction mechanisms.

### 2. Ultrastiff metamaterials generated through a multilayer strategy and topology optimization

*File: `Ultrastiff_metamaterials_generated_through_a_multilayer_strategy_and_t__W4394009839.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes a multilayer strategy combined with topology optimization to design lattice metamaterials that combine beam, plate and shell elements.

**Main notes:**

- The multilayer strategy enlarges and diversifies the design space.
- Beam-plate-shell-combined metamaterials emerge automatically with ultrahigh stiffness.
- Optimized designs show improved energy absorption under large deformation.
