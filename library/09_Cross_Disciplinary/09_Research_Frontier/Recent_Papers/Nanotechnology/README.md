### 1. Drug repurposing for cancer therapy

*File: `Drug_repurposing_for_cancer_therapy__W4394934376.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of drug repurposing, finding new uses for approved drugs, as a strategy for cancer treatment limited by drug resistance and toxicity.

**Main notes:**

- Repurposed drugs are cost-effective, proven safe and speed up development.
- Overviews methods used in drug repurposing.
- Describes antitumor properties of candidate drugs and how they target cancer hallmarks.

### 2. Immune evasion in cancer: mechanisms and cutting-edge therapeutic approaches

*File: `Immune_evasion_in_cancer_mechanisms_and_cutting_edge_therapeutic_appro__W4412745982.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of how tumors escape immune surveillance and what this means for immunotherapy.

**Main notes:**

- Covers tumor-induced immune suppression, checkpoint regulation, and genetic and epigenetic influences.
- Discusses the tumor microenvironment, metabolic reprogramming and tumor heterogeneity.
- Analyzes pathways including PD-1/PD-L1, CTLA-4, TGF-beta, NF-kB and cGAS-STING.
