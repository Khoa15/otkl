### 1. Nano based drug delivery systems: recent developments and future prospects

*File: `Nano_based_drug_delivery_systems_recent_developments_and_future_prospe__W2890167612.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of nanomedicine and nano-based drug delivery, where nanoscale materials serve as diagnostic tools or deliver therapeutics to specific sites in a controlled way.

**Main notes:**

- Nanotechnology offers site-specific, target-oriented delivery for chronic diseases.
- Covers applications for chemotherapeutic, biological and immunotherapeutic agents.
- Reviews improving efficacy of new and old drugs, including natural products, and selective diagnosis.
- Discusses opportunities and challenges of nanomedicines.

### 2. Review on Zinc Oxide Nanoparticles: Antibacterial Activity and Toxicity Mechanism

*File: `Review_on_Zinc_Oxide_Nanoparticles_Antibacterial_Activity_and_Toxicity__W1990681348.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of the antibacterial activity of ZnO nanoparticles, whose small size gives high specific surface area and reactivity. It stresses bactericidal and bacteriostatic mechanisms.

**Main notes:**

- Covers testing methods, UV illumination effects, particle size, concentration, morphology and defects.
- Discusses surface modification and minimum inhibitory concentration.
- Focuses on reactive oxygen species generation (H2O2, hydroxyl radicals, peroxide).
- Describes ZnO as a bio-safe material with photocatalytic properties.
