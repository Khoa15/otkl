### 1. Review of deep learning: concepts, CNN architectures, challenges, applications, future directions

*File: `Review_of_deep_learning_concepts_CNN_architectures_challenges_applicat__W3140854437.pdf`*

**Brief:** A broad review of deep learning that takes a holistic approach rather than covering a single aspect. It aims to be a starting point for understanding DL concepts, CNN architectures, challenges, applications and future directions.

**Main notes:**

- Describes deep learning as the dominant ML paradigm, often matching or beating humans on complex tasks.
- Cites applications in cybersecurity, NLP, bioinformatics, robotics and control, and medical information processing.
- Argues earlier reviews each tackled only one aspect of DL.
- Covers concepts, CNN architectures, challenges and future directions.

### 2. Second-generation PLINK: rising to the challenge of larger and richer datasets

*File: `Second_generation_PLINK_rising_to_the_challenge_of_larger_and_richer_d__W2099085143.pdf`*

**Brief:** A technical note on PLINK 1.9, a widely used open-source C/C++ toolset for genome-wide association studies and population genetics. It addresses the strain placed on the tool by steadily growing and richer datasets.

**Main notes:**

- PLINK 1 is an open-source C/C++ toolset for GWAS and population genetics.
- The work responds to ever-larger and richer genomic datasets.
- Published in GigaScience 2015, 4:7.
