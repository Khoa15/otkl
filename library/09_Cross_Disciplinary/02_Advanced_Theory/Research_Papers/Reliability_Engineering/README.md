### 1. How to tell the difference between a model and a digital twin

*File: `How_to_tell_the_difference_between_a_model_and_a_digital_twin__W3014048166.pdf`*

**Brief:** Discusses the vague and inconsistent use of the term digital twin across sectors and the risk that this leads to hype and backlash. It aims to clarify how a digital twin differs from an ordinary model.

**Main notes:**

- Digital twin is used for many things from manufacturing to city planning.
- Claimed benefits include design, optimisation, process control, virtual testing, predictive maintenance and lifetime estimation.
- Poor definitions risk the technology being dismissed as hype.

### 2. Mechanisms of energy absorption in special devices for use in earthquake resistant structures

*File: `Mechanisms_of_energy_absorption_in_special_devices_for_use_in_earthqua__W3131654573.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes separating the load-carrying function of a structure from its energy-absorbing function, using special devices to dissipate earthquake energy. Beam-column connections are unreliable under cyclic loading.

**Main notes:**

- Steel frame connections deteriorate from local flange instabilities.
- Reinforced concrete connections suffer diagonal tension and bond failures under alternating shear.
- Studies three essentially different energy-absorption mechanisms for feasibility.
