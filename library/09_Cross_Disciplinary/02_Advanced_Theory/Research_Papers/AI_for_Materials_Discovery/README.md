### 1. An autonomous laboratory for the accelerated synthesis of inorganic materials

*File: `An_autonomous_laboratory_for_the_accelerated_synthesis_of_inorganic_ma__W4389132715.pdf`*

**Brief:** Introduces the A-Lab, a robotic platform for solid-state synthesis of inorganic powders. It aims to close the gap between fast computational screening of new materials and slow experimental realization, using computation, literature data, machine learning and active learning to plan and interpret experiments.

**Main notes:**

- Over 17 days of continuous operation, the A-Lab realized 36 compounds out of 57 targets (oxides and phosphates).
- Targets were identified from ab initio phase-stability data from the Materials Project and Google DeepMind.
- Synthesis recipes were proposed by natural-language models trained on literature and optimized by thermodynamics-grounded active learning.
- Analysis of failed syntheses gives actionable suggestions for improving screening and synthesis design.

### 2. Recent advances and applications of machine learning in solid-state materials science

*File: `Recent_advances_and_applications_of_machine_learning_in_solid_state_ma__W2968923792.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of how machine learning is being applied to solid-state materials research. It surveys ML principles, descriptors and databases, then applications from stability and crystal structure prediction to property prediction.

**Main notes:**

- Introduces ML principles, algorithms, descriptors and materials databases.
- Covers ML approaches for discovering stable materials and predicting crystal structures.
- Discusses quantitative structure-property relationships and replacing first-principles methods with ML.
- Reviews active learning and surrogate-based optimization for rational design.
- Considers interpretability and physical understanding of ML models.
