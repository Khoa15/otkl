### 1. Generative AI

*File: `Generative_AI__W4386693657.pdf`*

**Brief:** A short catchword article introducing generative AI: computational techniques that generate seemingly new, meaningful content such as text, images or audio from training data. It discusses its impact on work and communication.

**Main notes:**

- Defines generative AI and cites examples such as Dall-E 2, GPT-4 and Copilot.
- Notes creative tasks once thought exclusively human can now be done by AI.
- Mentions use as intelligent question-answering and decision-support systems.

### 2. MetaGPT: Meta Programming for A Multi-Agent Collaborative Framework

*File: `MetaGPT_Meta_Programming_for_A_Multi_Agent_Collaborative_Framework__W4385963839.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents MetaGPT, a meta-programming framework for LLM-based multi-agent collaboration. It addresses logic inconsistencies and cascading hallucinations caused by naively chaining LLMs.

**Main notes:**

- Encodes Standardized Operating Procedures (SOPs) into prompt sequences.
- Uses an assembly-line paradigm assigning different roles to agents.
- Agents with human-like domain expertise verify intermediate results to reduce errors.
- Published at ICLR 2024.
