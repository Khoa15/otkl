### 2. Hybrid threshold adaptable quantum secret sharing scheme with reverse Huffman-Fibonacci-tree coding

*File: `Hybrid_threshold_adaptable_quantum_secret_sharing_scheme_with_reverse__W2508570029.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes a quantum secret sharing scheme that combines quantum entanglement with classical secret sharing to cut computational cost and bandwidth.

**Main notes:**

- Uses m-bonacci orbital angular momentum pump to prepare entangled states for eavesdropping detection
- Shares encoded via Lagrange interpolation polynomials and reverse Huffman-Fibonacci-tree coding
- Detects eavesdropping without joint quantum operations
- Supports dynamic changes such as unavailable channels or new participants
