### 2. Evidence for the utility of quantum computing before fault tolerance

*File: `Evidence_for_the_utility_of_quantum_computing_before_fault_tolerance__W4380627387.pdf`*

**Brief:** IBM experiment on a noisy 127-qubit processor measuring accurate expectation values beyond brute-force classical simulation.

**Main notes:**

- Enabled by improved coherence, calibration and noise characterization/control
- Accuracy checked against exactly verifiable circuits
- In strong entanglement, correct results where leading classical approximations fail
