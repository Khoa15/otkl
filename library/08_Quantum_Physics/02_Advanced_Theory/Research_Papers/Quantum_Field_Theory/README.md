### 1. A Weyl Fermion semimetal with surface Fermi arcs in the transition metal monopnictide TaAs class

*File: `A_Weyl_Fermion_semimetal_with_surface_Fermi_arcs_in_the_transition_met__W2261951110.pdf`*

**Brief:** Theoretical identification of a Weyl semimetal state in TaAs-type materials by first-principles calculations.

**Main notes:**

- Predicts bulk Weyl cones and surface Fermi arcs in TaAs
- Weyl state does not require fine-tuning of composition or magnetic order
- Condensed-matter realization of quantum-field-theory anomalies

### 2. Unique S-scheme heterojunctions in self-assembled TiO2/CsPbBr3 hybrids for CO2 photoreduction

*File: `Unique_S_scheme_heterojunctions_in_self_assembled_TiO2_CsPbBr3_hybrids__W3085189088.pdf`*

**Brief:** Develops TiO2/CsPbBr3 quantum dot nanofiber heterojunctions that improve photocatalytic CO2 reduction.

**Main notes:**

- Electrostatic self-assembly synthesis
- DFT and in-situ XPS support an internal electric field and S-scheme electron transfer
- CO2 reduction rate 9.02 vs 4.68 micromol/g/h for pristine TiO2
- 13CO2 tracer confirms CO2 as the product source
