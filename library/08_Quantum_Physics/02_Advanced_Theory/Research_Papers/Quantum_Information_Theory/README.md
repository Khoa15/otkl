### 1. Double-slit photoelectron interference in strong-field ionization of the neon dimer

*File: `Double_slit_photoelectron_interference_in_strong_field_ionization_of_t__W2790756470.pdf`*

**Brief:** Observes two-center interference in the photoelectron momentum distribution when a neon dimer is ionized by a strong laser field.

**Main notes:**

- Molecular-frame electron distribution measured in coincidence with ions
- Post-selection of ions chooses residual-ion symmetry
- Both gerade and ungerade interference types seen

### 2. E(3)-equivariant graph neural networks for data-efficient and accurate interatomic potentials

*File: `E_3_equivariant_graph_neural_networks_for_data_efficient_and_accurate__W4225405705.pdf`*

**Brief:** Introduces NequIP, an E(3)-equivariant neural network for learning interatomic potentials from ab-initio data.

**Main notes:**

- Uses equivariant convolutions on geometric tensors, not only scalars
- State-of-the-art accuracy on diverse molecules and materials
- Up to three orders of magnitude less training data needed
- Enables long-timescale molecular dynamics at high-order quantum chemistry accuracy
