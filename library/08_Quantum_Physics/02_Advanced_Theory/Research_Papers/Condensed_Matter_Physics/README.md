### 1. Three options for citation tracking: Google Scholar, Scopus and Web of Science

*File: `Three_options_for_citation_tracking_Google_Scholar_Scopus_and_Web_of_S__W1890493318.pdf`*

**Brief:** Observational study comparing citation counts from Web of Science, Scopus and Google Scholar. It tests whether their different publication coverage leads to different counts for oncology and condensed matter physics articles from 1993 and 2003.

**Main notes:**

- Compares three citation-tracking databases
- Uses articles from two disciplines (oncology, condensed matter physics) and two years (1993, 2003)
- Hypothesis: differing coverage yields different citation counts
- Not a physics paper; a library-science study

### 2. Topological nodal-line fermions in spin-orbit metal PbTaSe2

*File: `Topological_nodal_line_fermions_in_spin_orbit_metal_PbTaSe2__W2338782110.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reports theory and experiment showing topological nodal-line states in the non-centrosymmetric compound PbTaSe2. Nodal lines need crystalline symmetry protection and here are also characterized by an integer topological invariant.

**Main notes:**

- Nodal lines protected by mirror reflection symmetry
- Combines angle-resolved photoemission, first-principles simulation and topological analysis
- Spin-orbit coupling is strong in this compound
- Nodal lines carry an integer topological invariant
