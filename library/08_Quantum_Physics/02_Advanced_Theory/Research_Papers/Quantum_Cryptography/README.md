### 1. Fundamental limits of repeaterless quantum communications

*File: `Fundamental_limits_of_repeaterless_quantum_communications__W2273682834.pdf`*

**Brief:** Establishes ultimate point-to-point rates for quantum communication without repeaters, allowing unlimited two-way classical communication.

**Main notes:**

- Upper bound from the relative entropy of entanglement
- Introduces the 'teleportation stretching' technique
- Exact capacities for bosonic lossy channels, quantum-limited amplifiers, dephasing and erasure channels
- Gives the exact rate-loss tradeoff for any QKD protocol

### 2. Practical challenges in quantum key distribution

*File: `Practical_challenges_in_quantum_key_distribution__W2473315843.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of the obstacles to wide QKD adoption and the approaches addressing them.

**Main notes:**

- Challenges: secret key rate, distance, size, cost, practical security
- QKD promises unconditional security and is being commercially deployed
- Motivated by the key distribution problem for one-time-pad encryption
