### 1. Quantum Computing in the NISQ era and beyond

*File: `Quantum_Computing_in_the_NISQ_era_and_beyond__W2781738013.pdf`*

**Brief:** Preskill's essay on Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum technology: devices with 50-100 qubits whose noise limits circuit size.

**Main notes:**

- NISQ devices may beat classical computers on some tasks
- Useful for exploring many-body quantum physics
- A 100-qubit device will not change the world immediately
- Urges more accurate gates and eventually fault tolerance

### 2. Variational quantum algorithms

*File: `Variational_quantum_algorithms__W3111162498.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of variational quantum algorithms that use a parametrized quantum circuit trained by a classical optimizer, suited to near-term noisy hardware.

**Main notes:**

- Applications include simulating quantum systems and large-scale linear algebra
- Hybrid quantum-classical optimization structure
- Written by a large multi-institution group (Los Alamos, Google, Oxford, etc.)
