### 1. Cost function dependent barren plateaus in shallow parametrized quantum circuits

*File: `Cost_function_dependent_barren_plateaus_in_shallow_parametrized_quantu__W3136233239.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proves how the choice of cost function controls trainability of variational quantum algorithms.

**Main notes:**

- Global observables give exponentially vanishing gradients even for shallow circuits
- Local observables give at worst polynomially vanishing gradients for O(log n) depth
- Assumes alternating layered ansatz of local 2-designs
- Simulations up to 100 qubits with a quantum autoencoder
