### 1. Barren plateaus in quantum neural network training landscapes

*File: `Barren_plateaus_in_quantum_neural_network_training_landscapes__W2794444783.pdf`*

**Brief:** Shows that training parameterized quantum circuits from random initializations is hard for more than a few qubits. Gradients vanish exponentially with qubit number.

**Main notes:**

- Probability of a non-zero gradient is exponentially small in qubit count
- Linked to the 2-design property of random circuits
- Affects hybrid quantum-classical algorithms
- Calls for solutions beyond random circuit initial guesses

### 2. Ising formulations of many NP problems

*File: `Ising_formulations_of_many_NP_problems__W2039122980.pdf`*

**Brief:** Provides Ising-model mappings for many NP-complete and NP-hard problems, including all 21 of Karp's NP-complete problems. Aimed at adiabatic quantum optimization.

**Main notes:**

- Required number of spins is at most cubic in problem size
- Collects and extends mappings from partitioning, covering and satisfiability
- Based on encoding a solution in the ground state of a problem Hamiltonian
