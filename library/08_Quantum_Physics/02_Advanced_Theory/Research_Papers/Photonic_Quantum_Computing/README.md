### 1. Multiple ETL and HTL layers for high-performance lead-free CsSnCl3 perovskite solar cells

*File: `An_extensive_study_on_multiple_ETL_and_HTL_layers_to_design_and_simula__W4320491231.pdf`*

**Brief:** Simulation study of lead-free CsSnCl3 perovskite solar cell configurations using SCAPS-1D. It screens many electron and hole transport layers to find well-aligned device stacks.

**Main notes:**

- Uses the SCAPS-1D solar cell simulator
- Tests ETLs such as IGZO, SnO2, WS2, CeO2, TiO2, ZnO, C60, PCBM
- Tests HTLs such as Cu2O, CuO, NiO, V2O5, CuI, CuSCN, Spiro-MeOTAD, P3HT, PEDOT:PSS
- ZnO, TiO2, IGZO, WS2, PCBM and C60 ETLs identified as promising

### 2. Fusion-based quantum computation

*File: `Fusion_based_quantum_computation__W3122257190.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents fusion-based quantum computation (FBQC), a fault-tolerant model built from entangling measurements (fusions) on small constant-size entangled resource states. It is designed around primitives natural to photonic hardware.

**Main notes:**

- Probabilistic gates and errors handled directly by the error-correction protocol
- Ballistic scheme tolerates 10.4% loss per fusion (2.7% per photon)
- Higher threshold than earlier photonic schemes
- Architecture is modular with reduced classical processing
