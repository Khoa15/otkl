### 1. Circularly polarized light detection using chiral hybrid perovskite

*File: `Circularly_polarized_light_detection_using_chiral_hybrid_perovskite__W2941942848.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reports a circularly polarized light detector using chiral organic-inorganic hybrid perovskites without extra optical elements.

**Main notes:**

- Responsivity 797 mA/W
- Detectivity 7.1e11 Jones
- 3-dB frequency 150 Hz and one-month stability
- Flexible devices on PET with comparable performance

### 2. Smart nanoparticles for cancer therapy

*File: `Smart_nanoparticles_for_cancer_therapy__W4388275106.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of stimulus-responsive nanoparticles for targeted cancer drug delivery.

**Main notes:**

- Covers polymeric, liposomal, gold, iron oxide, quantum dot, MOF and other nanoparticles
- Responds to enzymes, pH, temperature, light and magnetism
- Tumor targeting via antibodies, peptides, transferrin, folic acid
- Biomedical review, not quantum optics
