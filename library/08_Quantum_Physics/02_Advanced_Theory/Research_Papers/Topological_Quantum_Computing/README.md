### 1. Majorana zero modes and topological quantum computation

*File: `Majorana_zero_modes_and_topological_quantum_computation__W1571552325.pdf`*

**Brief:** Perspective on Majorana zero modes in solid-state systems and their use in braiding-based topological quantum computation.

**Main notes:**

- Non-Abelian braiding statistics suitable for unitary gates
- Experimental signatures in semiconductor nanowires with superconducting proximity effect
- Also covers the 5/2 fractional quantum Hall state
- Describes proposed braiding schemes

### 2. Negative magnetoresistance without well-defined chirality in the Weyl semimetal TaP

*File: `Negative_magnetoresistance_without_well_defined_chirality_in_the_Weyl__W1260748775.pdf`*

**Brief:** Studies the Fermi surface of TaP and its negative longitudinal magnetoresistance, even though chirality is ill-defined there.

**Main notes:**

- Quantum oscillation spectra and band-structure calculations give Fermi-surface topology
- Banana-shaped electron and hole pockets surround Weyl point pairs
- Large negative magnetoresistance observed
- Can be affected by inhomogeneous current distribution
