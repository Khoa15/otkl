### 1. Generating optical orbital angular momentum at visible wavelengths using a plasmonic metasurface

*File: `Generating_optical_orbital_angular_momentum_at_visible_wavelengths_usi__W2114579353.pdf`*

**Brief:** Designs and demonstrates a plasmonic nano-antenna metasurface that converts circularly polarized light into light carrying orbital angular momentum.

**Main notes:**

- Uses spin-to-orbit coupling in space-variant plasmonic nanostructures
- Generates OAM of 2 units at visible wavelengths
- Conversion efficiency at least 1%
- Ultrathin OAM generator for sensing and communications

### 2. Zinc Oxide Nanostructures for NO2 Gas-Sensor Applications: A Review

*File: `Zinc_Oxide_Nanostructures_for_NO2_Gas_Sensor_Applications_A_Review__W2418356540.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of ZnO nanomaterial gas sensors for NO2 detection.

**Main notes:**

- Covers nanorods, nanowires, flowers, quantum dots, thin films, nanosheets
- Discusses sensing mechanisms, response and recovery time, selectivity, detection limit
- Factors: concentration, annealing, morphology, humidity, temperature
