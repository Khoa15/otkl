### 1. Benchmarking an 11-qubit quantum computer

*File: `Benchmarking_an_11_qubit_quantum_computer__W2924182316.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents an 11-qubit fully connected programmable trapped-ion computer built from 13 171Yb+ ions.

**Main notes:**

- Single-qubit fidelity 99.5%, two-qubit 97.5%, SPAM error 0.7%
- Bernstein-Vazirani success 78%, Hidden Shift 35%
- Claims outperformance of other available hardware at the time
