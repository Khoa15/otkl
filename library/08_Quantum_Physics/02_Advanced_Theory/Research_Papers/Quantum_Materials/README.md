### 1. The Open Quantum Materials Database (OQMD): assessing the accuracy of DFT formation energies

*File: `The_Open_Quantum_Materials_Database_OQMD_assessing_the_accuracy_of_DFT__W2278970271.pdf`*

**Brief:** Describes the OQMD, a high-throughput DFT database of nearly 300,000 calculations, and uses it to assess DFT formation energy accuracy.

**Main notes:**

- Database freely downloadable at oqmd.org
- Compared against 1,670 experimental formation energies
- Mean absolute error 0.096 eV/atom versus experiment
- Experiments themselves differ by 0.082 eV/atom, so much of the error may be experimental

### 2. Ti3C2 MXene co-catalyst on metal sulfide photo-absorbers for enhanced visible-light photocatalytic hydrogen production

*File: `Ti3C2_MXene_co_catalyst_on_metal_sulfide_photo_absorbers_for_enhanced__W2566202566.pdf`*

**Brief:** Uses Ti3C2 MXene nanoparticles as an earth-abundant co-catalyst replacing platinum for photocatalytic hydrogen production.

**Main notes:**

- DFT-guided design combined with hydrothermal integration with CdS
- H2 production 14,342 micromol/h/g
- Apparent quantum efficiency 40.1% at 420 nm
- Also works on ZnS and ZnxCd1-xS
