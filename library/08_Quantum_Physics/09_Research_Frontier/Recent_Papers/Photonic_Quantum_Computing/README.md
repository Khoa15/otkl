### 1. High-efficiency single-photon source above the loss-tolerant threshold for efficient linear optical quantum computing

*File: `High_efficiency_single_photon_source_above_the_loss_tolerant_threshold__W4408034515.pdf`*

**Brief:** Quantum dot single-photon source exceeding the 1/3 loss threshold needed for error-corrected photonic quantum computing.

**Main notes:**

- Photon loss is the main enemy of photonic quantum processing
- Tailored laser pulse excitation on a high-efficiency quantum dot coupled to a tunable cavity
- First on-demand indistinguishable source to pass the threshold, per the abstract's framing

### 2. Nonreciprocal Quantum Batteries

*File: `Nonreciprocal_Quantum_Batteries__W4398204453.pdf`*

**Brief:** Explores nonreciprocity engineered through reservoir engineering to improve quantum battery charging.

**Main notes:**

- Directed energy flow from charger to battery
- Fourfold increase in battery energy despite local dissipation
- Shared reservoir gives optimal conditions
- Works in stationary and overdamped regimes without precise time control
