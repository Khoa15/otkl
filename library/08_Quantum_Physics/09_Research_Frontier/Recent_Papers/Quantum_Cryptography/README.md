### 1. Quantum key distribution implemented with d-level time-bin entangled photons

*File: `Quantum_key_distribution_implemented_with_d_level_time_bin_entangled_p__W4406001279.pdf`*

**Brief:** Integrated photonic platform for generating and processing time-bin entangled qudits for high-dimensional QKD.

**Main notes:**

- Fiber-pigtailed on-chip interferometry in the telecom C band
- Picosecond-spaced time bins
- Demonstrates the BBM92 QKD protocol with qudits
- Targets phase instability and scalability issues

### 2. The Evolution of Quantum Secure Direct Communication: On the Road to the Qinternet

- Open challenges and network trends
