### 1. Fusion category symmetry. Part II. Categoriosities at c = 1 and beyond

*File: `Fusion_category_symmetry_Part_II_Categoriosities_at_c_1_and_beyond__W4400387459.pdf`*

**Brief:** Studies fusion category symmetries of 1+1D quantum field theories, focusing on c = 1 CFTs.

**Main notes:**

- Uses modular bootstrap, rational CFT and gauging
- c = 1 CFTs with group-like symmetry can have continuous fusion category symmetries
- Proves a Noether theorem relating them to non-local conserved currents
- New constraints on RG flows

### 2. Fusion category symmetry. Part I. Anomaly in-flow and gapped phases

*File: `Fusion_category_symmetry_Part_I_Anomaly_in_flow_and_gapped_phases__W4395071929.pdf`*

**Brief:** Studies non-invertible generalized symmetries in 1+1D QFT, their anomalies and gapped phases.

**Main notes:**

- Symmetry described by fusion categories
- Classifies gapped phases including new topological phases
- Connects to Turaev-Viro/Levin-Wen models in 2+1D
