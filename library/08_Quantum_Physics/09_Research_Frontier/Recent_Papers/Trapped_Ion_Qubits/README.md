### 1. Benchmarking a trapped-ion quantum computer with 30 qubits

*File: `Benchmarking_a_trapped_ion_quantum_computer_with_30_qubits__W4404140215.pdf`*

**Brief:** Benchmarks IonQ Forte, a 30-qubit single-chain trapped-ion computer.

**Main notes:**

- Direct randomized benchmarking over all 435 gate pairs
- Passes algorithmic qubit benchmarks up to #AQ 29
- System-level model predicts results but with out-of-model errors

### 2. Distributed quantum computing across an optical network link

*File: `Distributed_quantum_computing_across_an_optical_network_link__W4407158326.pdf`*

**Brief:** Demonstrates distributed computation between two photonically linked trapped-ion modules.

**Main notes:**

- Modules about 2 m apart
- Deterministic teleported CZ gate with 86% fidelity
- First distributed Grover search with several non-local gates
