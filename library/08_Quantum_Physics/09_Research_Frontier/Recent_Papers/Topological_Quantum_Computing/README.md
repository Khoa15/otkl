### 1. Anomalies of non-invertible symmetries in (3+1)d

*File: `Anomalies_of_non_invertible_symmetries_in_3_1_d__W4404277122.pdf`*

**Brief:** Studies anomalies of non-invertible symmetries in 3+1d via 4+1d topological gauge theories.

**Main notes:**

- Two anomaly levels: missing condensable loop excitations and Frobenius-Schur indicator
- Conditions for symmetric gapped phases
- Z4 defects host worldvolume theories

### 2. Signatures of Chiral Superconductivity in Rhombohedral Graphene

*File: `Signatures_of_chiral_superconductivity_in_rhombohedral_graphene__W4410596790.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reports signatures of chiral superconductivity in rhombohedral graphene.

**Main notes:**

- Chiral superconductors break time-reversal symmetry spontaneously
- May host Majorana fermions for fault-tolerant computing
- Long elusive in other candidates
