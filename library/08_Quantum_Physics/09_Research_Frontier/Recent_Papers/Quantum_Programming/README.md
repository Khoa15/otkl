### 2. Generalized charges, part II: Non-invertible symmetries and the symmetry TFT

*File: `Generalized_charges_part_II_Non_invertible_symmetries_and_the_symmetry__W4415192007.pdf`*

**Brief:** Shows q-charges of generalized symmetries are characterized by topological defects of the SymTFT.

**Main notes:**

- SymTFT is a (d+1)-dimensional topological field theory (sandwich construction)
- Defects form the Drinfeld center of the symmetry category
- Developed for 2d fusion categories and 3d fusion 2-categories
