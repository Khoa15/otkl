### 1. Better than classical? The subtle art of benchmarking quantum machine learning models

*File: `Better_than_classical_The_subtle_art_of_benchmarking_quantum_machine_l__W4392781618.pdf`*

**Brief:** Large-scale benchmark of quantum machine learning models against classical ones.

**Main notes:**

- Tests 12 QML models on 6 tasks forming 160 datasets
- Classical models outperform quantum classifiers out of the box
- Removing entanglement often performs as well or better
- Open-source PennyLane-based package
