### 2. Reconfiguring nucleation for CVD growth of twisted bilayer MoS2 with a wide range of twist angles

*File: `Reconfiguring_nucleation_for_CVD_growth_of_twisted_bilayer_MoS2_with_a__W4390951314.pdf`*

**Brief:** CVD strategy for directly synthesizing twisted bilayer MoS2 with controlled twist angles.

**Main notes:**

- Twist angles from 0 to 120 degrees
- Moire periodicity and interlayer coupling depend on twist angle
- Bilayer yield improved to 17.2%; density 28.9 pieces/mm2 by tuning gas flow and NaCl:MoO3 ratio
