### 1. Observation of quantum entanglement with top quarks at the ATLAS detector

*File: `Observation_of_quantum_entanglement_with_top_quarks_at_the_ATLAS_detec__W4402597137.pdf`*

**Brief:** ATLAS observation of spin entanglement in top-antitop pairs at the LHC.

**Main notes:**

- 13 TeV pp data, 140 fb^-1
- Observable D from angle between charged leptons
- Measured near production threshold
- Highest-energy observation of entanglement

### 2. Quantum-enhanced metrology with large Fock states

*File: `Quantum_enhanced_metrology_with_large_Fock_states__W4401973308.pdf`*

**Brief:** Generates Fock states of nearly 100 photons in a superconducting cavity and uses them for metrology.

**Main notes:**

- Programmable photon number filter
- Heisenberg-scaling approach with 40-photon states
- Maximum metrological gain 14.8 dB
