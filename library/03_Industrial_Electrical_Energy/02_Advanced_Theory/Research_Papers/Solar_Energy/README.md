### 1. Consensus statement for stability assessment and reporting for perovskite photovoltaics

*File: `Consensus_statement_for_stability_assessment_and_reporting_for_perovsk__W3002058265.pdf`*

**Brief:** A Nature Energy consensus statement on how to assess and report stability of emerging PV (organic, dye-sensitized, perovskite). Existing IEC qualification tests were designed for silicon.

**Main notes:**

- Module lifetime expectation in power plants is 20-25 years
- Emerging technologies do not yet meet this target and failure modes are poorly understood
- IEC 61215-type tests are unlikely to suit OPV, DSSC and perovskite cells

### 2. One-Year stable perovskite solar cells by 2D/3D interface engineering

*File: `One_Year_stable_perovskite_solar_cells_by_2D_3D_interface_engineering__W2621222172.pdf`*

**Brief:** Addresses poor operational stability of perovskite solar cells by engineering a 2D/3D perovskite junction using an aminovaleric-acid-based 2D layer on CH3NH3PbI3.

**Main notes:**

- Carbon-based architecture reaches 12.9% efficiency; standard mesoporous cells 14.6%
- 10 x 10 cm2 modules made by a fully printable industrial-scale process reach 11.2% efficiency
- Modules stable for over 10,000 h with zero loss under controlled conditions (as extracted)
