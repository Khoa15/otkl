### 1. Cost, environmental impact, and resilience of renewable energy under a changing climate: a review

*File: `Cost_environmental_impact_and_resilience_of_renewable_energy_under_a_c__W4307479757.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reviews solar, wind, biomass, hydropower and geothermal energy with respect to cost, climate impacts on renewables, environmental effects and decarbonization.

**Main notes:**

- Fossil fuels cause over 75% of greenhouse gas emissions and about 90% of CO2 emissions
- Climate change effects (heat, extreme winds, sea level rise, reduced precipitation) may affect renewables
- Notes the falling price of solar photovoltaic energy

### 2. Wind-Turbine and Wind-Farm Flows: A Review

*File: `Wind_Turbine_and_Wind_Farm_Flows_A_Review__W2974473399.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of the fluid mechanics of wind turbines and wind farms, centred on interactions with the turbulent atmospheric boundary layer (ABL) that limit performance prediction.

**Main notes:**

- ABL turbulence strongly affects turbine wakes and their superposition
- Wakes cause considerable power losses and fatigue loads in wind farms
- Complications include high Reynolds number, unsteadiness, thermal effects and terrain heterogeneity
