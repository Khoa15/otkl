### 1. Aqueous rechargeable zinc/sodium vanadate batteries with enhanced performance from simultaneous insertion of dual carriers

*File: `Aqueous_rechargeable_zinc_sodium_vanadate_batteries_with_enhanced_perf__W2801873687.pdf`*

**Brief:** Addresses the limited positive electrode choices for aqueous zinc-ion batteries. It reports a zinc/sodium vanadate system with simultaneous proton and zinc-ion insertion.

**Main notes:**

- Sodium vanadate hydrate nanobelts as cathode; zinc sulfate electrolyte with sodium sulfate additive
- Reversible capacity of 380 mAh/g with 82% retention over 1000 cycles
- Quasi-solid-state version is a candidate for flexible energy storage

### 2. Rechargeable aqueous zinc-manganese dioxide batteries with high energy and power densities

*File: `Rechargeable_aqueous_zinc_manganese_dioxide_batteries_with_high_energy__W2747631106.pdf`*

**Brief:** Tackles making zinc-manganese dioxide batteries rechargeable. It uses a mild-acidic aqueous zinc triflate electrolyte and a phase-transition electrode mechanism.

**Main notes:**

- Tunnel-structured MnO2 transforms to layered zinc-buserite on first discharge, allowing zinc intercalation
- Zinc/manganese triflate electrolyte forms a protective porous manganese oxide layer
- Capacity 225 mAh/g with 94% retention over 2000 cycles
- Pouch cell energy density 75.2 Wh/kg
