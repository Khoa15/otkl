### 1. A comprehensive review of DC fault protection methods in HVDC transmission systems

*File: `A_comprehensive_review_of_DC_fault_protection_methods_in_HVDC_transmis__W3158597070.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of DC fault detection, location and isolation techniques for CSC and VSC-based HVDC systems. VSC-HVDC is vulnerable to DC faults, making protection increasingly important.

**Main notes:**

- DC fault current reaches a large peak within a few milliseconds, so high-speed detection and isolation are required
- Protection of multi-terminal VSC-HVDC grids is particularly challenging
- Presents characteristics of DC faults in VSC-HVDC systems

### 2. Hydro, wind and solar power as a base for a 100% renewable energy supply for South and Central America

*File: `Hydro_wind_and_solar_power_as_a_base_for_a_100_renewable_energy_supply__W2601085257.pdf`*

**Brief:** Models a 100% renewable power system for South and Central America in 2030 with an hourly resolved model over 15 sub-regions. Four scenarios vary HVDC grid development and add desalination and power-to-gas.

**Main notes:**

- Renewables cover 1813 TWh of 2030 electricity demand plus 3.9 billion m3 desalination and 640 TWh(LHV) synthetic gas
- Existing hydro dams can act as virtual batteries, reducing storage needs
- Levelized cost of electricity falls from 62 to 56 EUR/MWh from decentralized to centralized grid
- Integrated scenario: LCOG 95 EUR/MWh(LHV), LCOW 0.91 EUR/m3
