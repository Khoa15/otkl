### 2. A comprehensive review on hybrid electric vehicles: architectures and components

*File: `A_comprehensive_review_on_hybrid_electric_vehicles_architectures_and_c__W2922137599.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of hybrid electric vehicles (HEVs), which combine an internal combustion engine with an electrical machine to improve fuel economy and cut emissions. It examines the components that determine HEV performance.

**Main notes:**

- Main components: energy storage system, motor, bidirectional converter and MPPT (for solar-powered HEVs)
- Reviews HEV architectures with advantages and disadvantages
- Motivated by fossil fuel consumption, environmental damage and rising fuel prices
