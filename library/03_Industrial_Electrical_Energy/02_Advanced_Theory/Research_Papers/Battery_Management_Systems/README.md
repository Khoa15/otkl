### 1. Applications of Lithium-Ion Batteries in Grid-Scale Energy Storage Systems

*File: `Applications_of_Lithium_Ion_Batteries_in_Grid_Scale_Energy_Storage_Sys__W3005112572.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of lithium-ion batteries as grid-level energy storage, covering operating mechanism, design and construction, advantages and disadvantages. It assesses how well they serve grid services.

**Main notes:**

- Batteries suit grid storage because of rapid response, modularity and flexible installation
- Lithium-ion offers high energy efficiency, long cycle life and relatively high energy density
- Grid services analysed include frequency regulation and peak shifting, among others

### 2. State of Charge Estimation for Lithium-Ion Batteries Using Model-Based and Data-Driven Methods: A Review

*File: `State_of_Charge_Estimation_for_Lithium_Ion_Batteries_Using_Model_Based__W2973538758.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of state-of-charge (SOC) estimation for lithium-ion batteries in electric vehicles. SOC estimation is a key function of the battery management system.

**Main notes:**

- Lithium-ion is favoured for EVs for high voltage, high energy density, low self-discharge and long life
- Covers both model-based and data-driven SOC estimation approaches
- Successful EV operation depends heavily on the battery management system
