### 1. Electric Power Systems in More and All Electric Aircraft: A Review

*File: `Electric_Power_Systems_in_More_and_All_Electric_Aircraft_A_Review__W3088679456.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of electric power systems for more-electric and all-electric aircraft, motivated by aviation greenhouse gas emissions. Electrification increases the complexity of aircraft power systems.

**Main notes:**

- Narrow and wide body aircraft cause over 75% of aviation GHG emissions
- Aviation was about 2.5% of US GHG emissions in 2018, with 4-5% annual air travel growth
- Trend is to eliminate all but electrical energy forms in aircraft power distribution

### 2. Power Electronics Technology for Large-Scale Renewable Energy Generation

*File: `Power_Electronics_Technology_for_Large_Scale_Renewable_Energy_Generati__W4324291204.pdf`*

**Brief:** Covers grid integration of large-scale renewable generation through power electronics, with focus on grid-connected wind, solar PV and energy storage. It gives latest statistics and developments.

**Main notes:**

- Converters give controllability but reduce system inertia in conventional grids
- Discusses technical issues and requirements for wind, PV and storage
- Covers control of individual converters (e.g. general current control) and system-level coordination
