### 1. Monitoring Training Load to Understand Fatigue in Athletes

*File: `Monitoring_Training_Load_to_Understand_Fatigue_in_Athletes__W2020287812.pdf`*

**Brief:** A sports-science review on monitoring training load to see whether athletes are adapting and to reduce risk of overreaching, illness or injury. It does not concern electrical power systems.

**Main notes:**

- Reviews external load tools (power output devices, time-motion analysis) and internal load measures (perceived effort, heart rate, blood lactate, training impulse)
- Few markers have strong scientific support and no single definitive marker exists
- Dissociation between external and internal load may reveal fatigue

### 2. Sisvar: a guide for its bootstrap procedures in multiple comparisons

*File: `Sisvar_a_Guide_for_its_Bootstrap_procedures_in_multiple_comparisons__W2033146070.pdf`*

**Brief:** A short review of the bootstrap multiple-comparison procedures in the Sisvar statistical analysis system. It is a statistics paper, not an electrical power topic.

**Main notes:**

- Bootstrap versions of Dunnett, Tukey, Student-Newman-Keuls and Scott-Knott tests are covered
- Reported to show greater power and better control of experimentwise type I error
- Aims to show advantages of Sisvar for comparing treatment means
