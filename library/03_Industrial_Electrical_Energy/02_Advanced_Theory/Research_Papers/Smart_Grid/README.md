### 1. Sustainable Energy Transition for Renewable and Low Carbon Grid Electricity Generation and Supply

*File: `Sustainable_Energy_Transition_for_Renewable_and_Low_Carbon_Grid_Electr__W4220773259.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reviews energy transition strategies and proposes a roadmap for sustainable electricity generation and supply in line with the Paris Agreement 1.5 C goal.

**Main notes:**

- Fossil fuels supplied 61.3% of global electricity in 2020
- Strategies: demand-side energy savings, generation efficiency, and substitution of fossil fuels by renewables and low-carbon nuclear
- Frames sustainable energy development as key to global sustainability
