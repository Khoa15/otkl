### 1. On the accuracy of turbulence models for CFD simulations of vertical axis wind turbines

*File: `On_the_accuracy_of_turbulence_models_for_CFD_simulations_of_vertical_a__W2945441518.pdf`*

**Brief:** Compares seven common eddy-viscosity turbulence models, plus inviscid modeling, for CFD of vertical axis wind turbines (VAWTs). Models are validated against three experimental sets.

**Main notes:**

- Models: Spalart-Allmaras, RNG k-epsilon, realizable k-epsilon, SST k-omega, SST with intermittency (SSTI), k-kl-omega, transition SST
- Validation covers three VAWTs across dynamic stall to highly rotational flow regimes
- Focus on turbine wake and power performance

### 2. RainNet v1.0: a convolutional neural network for radar-based precipitation nowcasting

*File: `RainNet_v1_0_a_convolutional_neural_network_for_radar_based_precipitat__W3009216045.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents RainNet, a deep convolutional network inspired by U-Net and SegNet for radar-based precipitation nowcasting. It is a meteorology paper, not a power system one.

**Main notes:**

- Trained to predict continuous precipitation intensity at a 5 min lead time
- Uses several years of German Weather Service radar composites, 900 km x 900 km at 1 km and 5 min resolution
- Independent verification experiments are reported
