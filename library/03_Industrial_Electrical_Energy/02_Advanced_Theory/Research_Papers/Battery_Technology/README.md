### 1. A reflection on lithium-ion battery cathode chemistry

*File: `A_reflection_on_lithium_ion_battery_cathode_chemistry__W3013252775.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review article looking back at how fundamental solid-state chemistry and physics led to modern lithium-ion cathodes, written after the 2019 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. It covers three major categories of oxide cathodes.

**Main notes:**

- Lithium-ion batteries power portable electronics, vehicle electrification and are entering utility use
- Their dominance stems from higher energy density enabled by high-energy electrode materials
- Basic science in the 1970s and 1980s was central to cathode discovery and design
- Ends with a personal perspective on the future of the field

### 2. High rate and stable cycling of lithium metal anode

*File: `High_rate_and_stable_cycling_of_lithium_metal_anode__W2047775546.pdf`*

**Brief:** Addresses dendrite growth and limited Coulombic efficiency that block practical lithium metal anodes. It shows that a highly concentrated ether electrolyte enables stable high-rate cycling.

**Main notes:**

- Electrolyte is 4 M lithium bis(fluorosulfonyl)imide in 1,2-dimethoxyethane
- Li|Li cell cycled at 10 mA/cm2 for over 6,000 cycles
- Cu|Li cell cycled at 4 mA/cm2 for over 1,000 cycles, average Coulombic efficiency 98.4% (up to 99.1%)
- Performance attributed to increased solvent coordination and available lithium ion concentration
