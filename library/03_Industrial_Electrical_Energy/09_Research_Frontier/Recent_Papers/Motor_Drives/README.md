### 1. EEG-based brain-computer interface enables real-time robotic hand control at individual finger level

*File: `EEG_based_brain_computer_interface_enables_real_time_robotic_hand_cont__W4411829903.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents a noninvasive EEG brain-computer interface that decodes individual finger movement or imagery to drive a robotic hand. It is a neuroengineering paper, not a motor drive one.

**Main notes:**

- Study with 21 able-bodied experienced BCI users
- Real-time decoding accuracy 80.56% for two-finger and 60.61% for three-finger motor imagery tasks
- Uses a deep neural network with fine-tuning
