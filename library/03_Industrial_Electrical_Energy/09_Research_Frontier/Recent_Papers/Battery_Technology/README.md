### 1. Innovative Solutions for High-Performance Silicon Anodes in Lithium-Ion Batteries: Overcoming Challenges and Real-World

*File: `Innovative_Solutions_for_High_Performance_Silicon_Anodes_in_Lithium_Io__W4395082452.pdf`*

**Brief:** A review of strategies to make silicon anodes practical in lithium-ion batteries, addressing volume expansion and unstable SEI.

**Main notes:**

- Si/C composites and multidimensional nanostructures tackle volume expansion and unstable SEI
- Artificial SEI layers, prelithiation and binders improve Coulombic efficiency and reduce capacity loss
- Analyses scalability and commercial viability of Si-anode technologies

### 2. Interfacial self-healing polymer electrolytes for long-cycle solid-state lithium-sulfur batteries

*File: `Interfacial_self_healing_polymer_electrolytes_for_long_cycle_solid_sta__W4390670057.pdf`*

**Brief:** Targets limited ion conductivity, large interfacial resistance and lithium dendrites in solid-state Li-metal batteries with a self-healing poly(ether-urethane) polymer electrolyte.

**Main notes:**

- Dynamic covalent disulfide bonds and hydrogen bonds give interfacial self-healing and good contact
- Li||Li symmetric cells cycle stably for more than 6000 h
- Solid-state Li-S battery shows prolonged cycling life (value cut off in extract)
