### 1. Manufacturing of non-grain-oriented electrical steels: review

*File: `Manufacturing_of_non_grain_oriented_electrical_steels_review__W4399321075.pdf`*

**Brief:** A critical review of non-grain-oriented electrical steel (NGOES), a soft magnetic material used in generators, motors and appliances.

**Main notes:**

- NGOES has high electrical resistivity, good saturation magnetization and minimal magnetostriction
- Magnetic properties depend on metallurgical factors such as Si content and grain size
- Expected to be a driver of the future electric power consumption market

### 2. Temperature Prediction and Performance Comparison of Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motors Using Different Machine Learnin

*File: `Temperature_Prediction_and_Performance_Comparison_of_Permanent_Magnet__W4401493479.pdf`*

**Brief:** Uses machine learning to predict permanent magnet synchronous motor (PMSM) temperature, replacing costly direct sensing for early failure detection.

**Main notes:**

- Four regressors compared: Linear, K-Nearest Neighbor, XGBoost, AdaBoost
- KNN regressor reached 99.65% training and 98.72% test accuracy
- RMSE 2.16, R2 score 98.72 and cross-validated R2 97.77%
