### 1. Gain and Phase: Decentralized Stability Conditions for Power Electronics-Dominated Power Systems

*File: `Gain_and_Phase_Decentralized_Stability_Conditions_for_Power_Electronic__W4393033631.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes decentralized stability conditions for multi-converter systems combining the small gain and small phase theorems.

**Main notes:**

- Compares individual converter dynamics with network dynamics in an open-loop, decentralized way
- Handles heterogeneous grid-following and grid-forming converters and synchronous generators
- Claimed to be significantly less conservative than passivity-based conditions

### 2. Wireless power and information dual transfer system via magnetically coupled resonators

*File: `Wireless_power_and_information_dual_transfer_system_via_magnetically_c__W4390675426.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes sending data over a medium-range wireless power transfer link by modulating it on the inverter/rectifier DC side.

**Main notes:**

- Uses the inverter/rectifier as a frequency mixer so data rides on a sideband of the power carrier
- Prototype at 6.78 MHz transfers 45 W across one meter at 62% efficiency
- Data rate 60 kb/s
