### 1. Accurate predictions on small data with a tabular foundation model

*File: `Accurate_predictions_on_small_data_with_a_tabular_foundation_model__W4406170795.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents TabPFN, a transformer-based tabular foundation model for small datasets. Machine learning paper, not electrical transformers.

**Main notes:**

- Outperforms prior methods on datasets up to 10,000 samples
- In 2.8 s it beats an ensemble of strong baselines tuned for 4 h in classification
- Gradient-boosted trees had dominated tabular data for 20 years

### 2. Learning to (Learn at Test Time): RNNs with Expressive Hidden States

*File: `Learning_to_Learn_at_Test_Time_RNNs_with_Expressive_Hidden_States__W4400435102.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes Test-Time Training layers whose hidden state is itself a model updated by self-supervised learning. Machine learning paper.

**Main notes:**

- Two instantiations: TTT-Linear and TTT-MLP
- Evaluated at 125M to 1.3B parameters against Transformer and Mamba
- Like Transformers, they keep reducing perplexity with more tokens
