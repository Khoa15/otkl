### 1. Extension of pole differential current based relaying for bipolar LCC HVDC lines

*File: `Extension_of_pole_differential_current_based_relaying_for_bipolar_LCC__W4410198067.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes fast line protection for LCC-HVDC using pole differential currents from single- and double-ended transient current measurements.

**Main notes:**

- Analyses DC line equivalent circuit to define local and global pole differential currents
- Criterion developed for bipolar and monopolar operation modes
- Validated through simulation

### 2. Walrus optimizer-based optimal fractional order PID control for performance enhancement of offshore wind farms

*File: `Walrus_optimizer_based_optimal_fractional_order_PID_control_for_perfor__W4401176692.pdf`*

**Brief:** Uses the Walrus Optimization algorithm to tune fractional-order PID controllers in the converter interfaces of an offshore wind farm connected by VSC-HVDC.

**Main notes:**

- Studied system has onshore and offshore VSC stations linked by HVDC
- Reported to outperform GA and PSO
- Improves system stability and speeds recovery after disturbances (text partly cut off)
