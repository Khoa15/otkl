### 1. Progress in satellite quantum key distribution

*File: `Progress_in_satellite_quantum_key_distribution__W2734851509.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of satellite-based quantum key distribution (QKD), which extends range beyond ground-based limits set by atmospheric and fibre losses. It covers protocols, infrastructure, technical challenges and ongoing initiatives.

**Main notes:**

- Ground-based QKD has a distance limit
- Satellites with high-quality optical links can extend range
- Surveys protocols, infrastructure and worldwide initiatives
