### 1. Heart Rate Variability - A Historical Perspective

*File: `Heart_Rate_Variability_A_Historical_Perspective__W2111772099.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of the history of heart rate variability (HRV), the beat-to-beat variation in heart rate or R-R interval, and its use as a clinical and investigational tool. It notes the synchrony of heart rate fluctuations with respiration.

**Main notes:**

- HRV is defined as beat-to-beat variation in heart rate or R-R interval
- Heart rate rises during inspiration, showing synchrony with respiration
- Historical overview of HRV as a clinical and research tool

### 2. Machine learning using intrinsic genomic signatures for rapid classification of novel pathogens: COVID-19 case study

*File: `Machine_learning_using_intrinsic_genomic_signatures_for_rapid_classifi__W3019119825.pdf`*

**Brief:** Applies machine learning to genomic signatures to classify novel pathogens quickly, using the 2019 coronavirus (COVID-19) as a case study. It was written when the outbreak had reached about 30 countries.

**Main notes:**

- Targets rapid classification of a novel pathogen from genome sequences
- Case study on COVID-19 as of February 2020
- Compared in the abstract with SARS coronavirus
