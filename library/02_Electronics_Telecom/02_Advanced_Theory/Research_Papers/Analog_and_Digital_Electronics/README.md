### 1. Observation of hybrid higher-order skin-topological effect in non-Hermitian topolectrical circuits

*File: `Observation_of_hybrid_higher_order_skin_topological_effect_in_non_Herm__W4226403469.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reports experiments on non-reciprocal 2D and 3D topolectrical circuit lattices that realize a hybrid higher-order skin-topological effect. It combines topological boundary protection with the non-Hermitian skin effect.

**Main notes:**

- Skin effect acts only on topological boundary modes, not bulk modes
- Experiments use specially designed non-reciprocal circuit lattices (2D and 3D)
- Shows non-reciprocal pumping together with topological localization

### 2. The AIM Photonics MPW: A Highly Accessible Cutting Edge Technology for Rapid Prototyping of Photonic Integrated Circuits

*File: `The_AIM_Photonics_MPW_A_Highly_Accessible_Cutting_Edge_Technology_for__W2969987856.pdf`*

**Brief:** Describes a silicon photonics multi-project wafer (MPW) service from AIM Photonics, aimed at lowering the barrier to high-quality photonic integrated circuit fabrication. It is presented as the first such MPW in a state-of-the-art 300 mm fabrication facility.

**Main notes:**

- Access to quality silicon photonics has been limited to vertically integrated or well-resourced companies
- R&D hubs release process design kits and MPW programs to lower the barrier
- First silicon photonics MPW service in a 300 mm facility
