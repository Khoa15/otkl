### 2. The Impact of Holding Voltage of Transient Voltage Suppressor (TVS) on Signal Integrity of Microelectronics System With

*File: `The_Impact_of_Holding_Voltage_of_Transient_Voltage_Suppressor_TVS_on_S__W3136413608.pdf`*

**Brief:** Examines how the holding voltage of a transient voltage suppressor affects signal integrity after system-level ESD and EFT/burst tests. A TVS with holding voltage below the IC operating voltage can leave the system disrupted.

**Main notes:**

- TVS devices protect PCB systems against ESD and EFT/burst events
- Signal integrity can be destroyed if holding voltage is below the CMOS IC operating voltage
- Compares TVS devices with different holding voltages
