### 1. A deep learning system for detecting diabetic retinopathy across the disease spectrum

*File: `A_deep_learning_system_for_detecting_diabetic_retinopathy_across_the_d__W3164440025.pdf`*

**Brief:** Develops DeepDR, a deep learning system that detects early-to-late stages of diabetic retinopathy for retinal screening. It performs image quality assessment, lesion detection and grading.

**Main notes:**

- Trained on 466,247 fundus images from 121,342 patients
- Local evaluation set: 200,136 images from 52,004 patients
- Plus external evaluation sets (details truncated)

### 2. Identifying degradation patterns of lithium ion batteries from impedance spectroscopy using machine learning

*File: `Identifying_degradation_patterns_of_lithium_ion_batteries_from_impedan__W3010408268.pdf`*

**Brief:** Combines electrochemical impedance spectroscopy with Gaussian process machine learning to forecast state of health and remaining useful life of Li-ion batteries.

**Main notes:**

- Over 20,000 EIS spectra of commercial batteries collected
- Varied states of health, states of charge and temperatures
- Described as the largest dataset of its kind
