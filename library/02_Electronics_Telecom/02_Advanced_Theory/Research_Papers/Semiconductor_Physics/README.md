### 1. Exciton physics and device application of two-dimensional transition metal dichalcogenide semiconductors

*File: `Exciton_physics_and_device_application_of_two_dimensional_transition_m__W2885309566.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of optical and optoelectronic properties of 2D transition metal dichalcogenides such as MoS2 and WSe2, emphasizing excitons and devices.

**Main notes:**

- Direct band gaps in infrared and visible ranges with strong light-matter coupling
- Excitons are tightly bound and dominate optical response at room temperature
- Covers bright, dark, localized and interlayer excitons

### 2. Expanding frontiers in materials chemistry and physics with multiple anions

*File: `Expanding_frontiers_in_materials_chemistry_and_physics_with_multiple_a__W2791003600.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of mixed-anion compounds (such as oxyhalides and oxyhydrides) as a new materials platform beyond single-oxide compounds.

**Main notes:**

- Focuses mainly on oxide-derived materials
- Covers recent progress, status, prospects and challenges
- Discusses the roles that multiple anions play
