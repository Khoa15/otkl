### 1. Integrated Sensing and Communications: Toward Dual-Functional Wireless Networks for 6G and Beyond

*File: `Integrated_Sensing_and_Communications_Toward_Dual_Functional_Wireless__W3194620979.pdf`*

**Brief:** IEEE JSAC special-issue overview of integrated sensing and communications (ISAC) as a key feature of the 6G radio access network. It reviews background, applications and state of the art, treating the dense cell infrastructure as a perceptive network.

**Main notes:**

- ISAC is presented as an emerging 6G RAN feature
- Exploits dense cell infrastructure for sensing as well as communication
- Comprehensive review of background, applications and state-of-the-art work
