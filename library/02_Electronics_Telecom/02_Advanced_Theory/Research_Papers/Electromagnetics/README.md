### 1. A guidance to intelligent metamaterials and metamaterials intelligence

*File: `A_guidance_to_intelligent_metamaterials_and_metamaterials_intelligence__W4406951686.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of the two-way interaction between metamaterials and artificial intelligence. AI can act as a fast surrogate electromagnetic simulator and drive self-driving metamaterial applications, while metamaterials can enable wave-based analogue computing.

**Main notes:**

- AI serves as a materials-savvy surrogate EM simulator
- Applications named include invisibility cloak, imaging, detection and wireless communication
- Metamaterials offer wave-based analogue computing complementary to electronic computing

### 2. Intelligent Reflecting Surface Aided Multigroup Multicast MISO Communication Systems

*File: `Intelligent_Reflecting_Surface_Aided_Multigroup_Multicast_MISO_Communi__W2973110004.pdf`*

**Brief:** Studies downlink multigroup multicast communication assisted by an intelligent reflecting surface (IRS). The IRS adjusts reflection phase shifts to strengthen desired signals or suppress interference.

**Main notes:**

- IRS provides massive-MIMO-like gains with low-cost passive elements
- Setting is downlink multigroup multicast with a multi-antenna transmitter
- Reflection coefficients are tuned to reshape the propagation environment
