### 1. Generative AI Agents with Large Language Model for Satellite Networks via a Mixture of Experts Transmission

*File: `Generative_AI_Agents_With_Large_Language_Model_for_Satellite_Networks__W4402473949.pdf`*

**Brief:** Develops generative AI agents with LLMs to formulate satellite network models and applies a mixture-of-experts approach to design transmission for 6G satellite networks.

**Main notes:**

- Transmission interference hurts performance
- Uses generative AI agents plus MoE

### 2. Multi-Satellite MIMO Systems for Direct User-Satellite Communications: A Survey

*File: `Multi_Satellite_MIMO_Systems_for_Direct_Satellite_to_Device_Communicat__W4401879495.pdf`*

- Multi-satellite MIMO addresses the link budget
- In-depth review of existing work
