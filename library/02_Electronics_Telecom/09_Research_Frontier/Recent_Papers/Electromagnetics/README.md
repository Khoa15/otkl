### 1. MOFs-Derived Strategy and Ternary Alloys Regulation in Flower-Like Magnetic-Carbon Microspheres with Broadband Electroma

*File: `MOFs_Derived_Strategy_and_Ternary_Alloys_Regulation_in_Flower_Like_Mag__W4400587329.pdf`*

**Brief:** Develops MOF-derived CoNiM@C microspheres (M = Cu, Zn, Fe, Mn) for electromagnetic wave absorption.

**Main notes:**

- CoNiMn@C flower-like microspheres reach 5.8 GHz effective absorption bandwidth
- Achieved at only 2.0 mm thickness
- Built with magnetic alloy-carbon heterogeneous interfaces

### 2. TESSERA: Temporal Embeddings of Surface Spectra for Earth Representation and Analysis

*File: `TESSERA_Temporal_Embeddings_of_Surface_Spectra_for_Earth_Representatio__W4414691205.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents TESSERA, a pixel-wise foundation model for Earth observation time series from Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2, avoiding information loss from compositing.

**Main notes:**

- Satellite time series are irregular due to orbits and clouds
- Compositing loses vegetation phenology information
- Multi-modal pixel-wise foundation model
