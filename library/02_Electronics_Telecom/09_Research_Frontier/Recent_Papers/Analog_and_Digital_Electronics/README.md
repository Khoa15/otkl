### 1. Powering AI at the edge: A robust, memristor-based binarized neural network with near-memory computing and miniaturized

*File: `Powering_AI_at_the_edge_A_robust_memristor_based_binarized_neural_netw__W4391222208.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents a memristor-based binarized neural network designed to work with unstable power from an energy harvester. It avoids analog in-memory computing that needs stable, precise supply.

**Main notes:**

- Memristor networks are energy-efficient and could be self-powered
- Most analog in-memory designs need a stable power supply
- Uses near-memory computing with a miniaturized solar cell

### 2. VO2 memristor-based frequency converter with in-situ synthesize and mix for wireless internet-of-things

*File: `VO2_memristor_based_frequency_converter_with_in_situ_synthesize_and_mi__W4391930415.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reports a VO2 memristor-based frequency converter that synthesizes and mixes signals in one component for wireless IoT, instead of separate CMOS synthesize and mix circuits.

**Main notes:**

- Frequency converters synthesize signals and mix them with sensor data
- Conventional separate CMOS circuits add latency and energy use
- Memristor approach combines synthesis and mixing
