### 1. 3D printing using concrete extrusion: A roadmap for research

*File: `3D_printing_using_concrete_extrusion_A_roadmap_for_research__W2808508889.pdf`*

**Brief:** Roadmap paper on 3D concrete printing (3DCP) by extrusion, covering what research is needed for the technology to mature. It addresses the gap between printing processes and the rheology and hardened properties of printable cementitious materials.

**Main notes:**

- Keywords include concrete printing, extrusion, rheology and hardened properties
- Authors come from universities, a concrete research centre, NIST and a 3DCP company
- Frames research needs for the process rather than reporting one experiment

### 2. Titanium dioxide nanoparticles: a review of current toxicological data

*File: `Titanium_dioxide_nanoparticles_a_review_of_current_toxicological_data__W2100168611.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of the toxicology of TiO2 nanoparticles, which are made worldwide in large quantities. It summarizes exposure routes and biological effects and points out where data are missing.

**Main notes:**

- Inhalation is the main workplace exposure route; translocation from lung and gut to organs appears low
- Most dermal studies report no penetration of the stratum corneum
- Intravenous exposure can cause lesions in liver, spleen, kidneys and brain, mostly at very high doses
- Epidemiological data are lacking; long-term rat inhalation studies reported lung tumors
