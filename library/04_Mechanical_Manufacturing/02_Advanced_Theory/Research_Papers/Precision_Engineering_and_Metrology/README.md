### 1. Probing 10 uK stability and residual drifts in the cross-polarized dual-mode stabilization of single-crystal ultrahigh-Q

*File: `Probing_10_K_stability_and_residual_drifts_in_the_cross_polarized_dual__W2906812762.pdf`*

**Brief:** Studies thermal stability of an MgF2 microresonator using cross-polarized dual-mode beat frequency metrology as a temperature sensor.

**Main notes:**

- Beat frequency reflects polarization-dependent thermorefractivity of the crystal
- Examines correlation between beat frequency and temperature over time, and its spectral noise
- Suggests stabilization could reach the fundamental thermal noise limit
