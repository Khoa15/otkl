### 1. Recent advances in biodegradable polymers for sustainable applications

*File: `Recent_advances_in_biodegradable_polymers_for_sustainable_applications__W4292296492.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of biodegradable polymers as alternatives to non-biodegradable plastics such as polyethylene, polypropylene and PVC. It covers production, composites, blending, modeling and environmental fate.

**Main notes:**

- Production routes include chemical treatment, microorganisms and enzymes
- Discusses environmental fate and assessment of these polymers
- Examines structure-property-behavior relationships before, during and after use

### 2. The success story of graphite as a lithium-ion anode material

*File: `The_success_story_of_graphite_as_a_lithium_ion_anode_material_fundamen__W3023001541.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of graphite as the lithium-ion intercalation host in the negative electrode, still used in most commercial cells after about 30 years. It fills the lack of a dedicated review on graphite.

**Main notes:**

- Covers the de-/lithiation mechanism fundamentals
- Discusses overcome and remaining challenges, including fast charging and recycling
- Covers natural versus synthetic graphite trade-offs and recent silicon (oxide) composites
