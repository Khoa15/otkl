### 1. PhysiCell: an Open Source Physics-Based Cell Simulator for 3-D Multicellular Systems

*File: `PhysiCell_An_open_source_physics_based_cell_simulator_for_3_D_multicel__W2949797623.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents PhysiCell, an open source agent-based simulator of many interacting cells in a dynamic tissue microenvironment. It couples a multi-substrate biotransport solver with cell phenotype.

**Main notes:**

- Built-in models for cell cycling, apoptosis, necrosis, volume changes, mechanics and motility
- C++ code with minimal dependencies, parallelized with OpenMP
- Performance scales linearly with cell count; 10^5 to 10^6 cells feasible on a quad-core desktop

### 2. Turbulence and Cavitation Suppression by Quaternary Ammonium Salt Additives

*File: `Turbulence_and_Cavitation_Suppression_by_Quaternary_Ammonium_Salt_Addi__W2804341996.pdf`*

**Brief:** Identifies how quaternary ammonium salt deposit control additives change the rheology of cavitating turbulent flow in fuel injectors, raising volumetric efficiency of clean injectors.

**Main notes:**

- SANS shows reverse cylindrical micelles form with the additive, versus reverse spherical micelles in traditional fuel
- Micro-CT in nozzle replicas shows higher liquid fraction in cavitation regions
- X-ray phase contrast imaging shows flow stabilization near vortex cavitation
- CFD with viscoelastic characteristics reproduces the effect and shows viscoelasticity can reduce turbulence
