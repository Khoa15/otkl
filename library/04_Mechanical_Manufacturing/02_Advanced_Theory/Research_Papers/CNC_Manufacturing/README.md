### 1. Artificial intelligence for automatic prediction of required surface roughness by monitoring wear on face mill teeth

*File: `Artificial_intelligence_for_automatic_prediction_of_required_surface_r__W2773444615.pdf`*

**Brief:** Uses AI models to predict surface roughness Ra in face milling from main drive power and tool wear VB. The aim is to change cutting inserts at the right time using real-time CNC data.

**Main notes:**

- Tested random forest, multilayer perceptron, regression trees and radial basis function models
- Random forest was most accurate, followed by regression trees; both beat MLP and RBF
- Can estimate the maximum tool wear, machining time and cutting power needed for a given roughness
- Random forest is easy to tune and gives visual information

### 2. Robot machining: recent development and future research issues

*File: `Robot_machining_recent_development_and_future_research_issues__W2051410726.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of robot machining research, which began in the 1990s but has not yet reached its potential versus CNC machines. It groups the work and suggests future directions.

**Main notes:**

- Categories: system development, path planning, vibration/chatter analysis with tracking and compensation, dynamic and stiffness modeling
- Suggested future work: machining efficiency analysis, stiffness-map-based path planning, robotic arm link optimization
- Also suggests planning and scheduling for a line of machining robots
