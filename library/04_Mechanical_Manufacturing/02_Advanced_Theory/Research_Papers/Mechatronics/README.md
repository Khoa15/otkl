### 1. Elastic straining of free-standing monolayer graphene

*File: `Elastic_straining_of_free_standing_monolayer_graphene__W2999123343.pdf`*

**Brief:** Measures the elastic properties and stretchability of CVD single-crystal monolayer graphene using in situ tensile tests in a scanning electron microscope.

**Main notes:**

- Young's modulus close to 1 TPa, matching theory
- Engineering tensile strength about 50-60 GPa
- Sample-wide elastic strain up to about 6%, even with edge defects

### 2. The exoskeleton expansion: improving walking and running economy

*File: `The_exoskeleton_expansion_improving_walking_and_running_economy__W3012841673.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of lower-limb exoskeletons that reduce the metabolic cost of walking and running, which first beat the 'metabolic cost barrier' in 2013.

**Main notes:**

- Analyzed literature through December 2019 and found 23 studies showing improved economy
- Key innovations: physiologically informed joint targeting, off-board actuators for prototyping controllers, active and passive mechatronic designs, better human-exoskeleton interfaces
- Highlights emerging trends and next grand challenges
