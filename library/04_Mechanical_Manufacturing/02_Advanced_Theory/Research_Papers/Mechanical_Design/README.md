### 1. Features of 20 133 UK patients in hospital with covid-19 using the ISARIC WHO Clinical Characterisation Protocol

*File: `Features_of_20_133_UK_patients_in_hospital_with_covid_19_using_the_ISA__W3026764413.pdf`*

**Brief:** Prospective cohort study characterizing clinical features and in-hospital mortality risk factors of covid-19 inpatients in the UK during the first wave. (Topic is clinical, not mechanical design.)

**Main notes:**

- 20,133 inpatients in 208 acute care hospitals, 6 Feb to 19 Apr 2020
- Outcomes: admission to critical care and in-hospital mortality
- Data collected with an ISARIC/WHO case report form with at least two weeks follow-up

### 2. TurkPrime.com: A versatile crowdsourcing data acquisition platform for the behavioral sciences

*File: `TurkPrime_com_A_versatile_crowdsourcing_data_acquisition_platform_for__W2342281051.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents TurkPrime, a browser-based research platform that integrates with Amazon Mechanical Turk to make common behavioral-science tasks easier. (Topic is social science tooling.)

**Main notes:**

- Supports excluding past participants, longitudinal studies, editing running studies and automated approval
- Bulk e-mails and bonuses, dropout monitoring and enhanced sampling options
- Free research platform aimed at improving data quality and saving time
