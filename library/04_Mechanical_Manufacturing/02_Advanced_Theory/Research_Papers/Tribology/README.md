### 1. Influence of tribology on global energy consumption, costs and emissions

*File: `Influence_of_tribology_on_global_energy_consumption_costs_and_emission__W2750857672.pdf`*

**Brief:** Calculates the global impact of friction and wear on energy use, cost and CO2 across transport, manufacturing, power generation and residential sectors.

**Main notes:**

- About 23% (119 EJ) of world energy use originates from tribological contacts
- 20% (103 EJ) overcomes friction and 3% (16 EJ) remanufactures worn parts
- New surface, materials and lubrication technologies can reduce these losses
