### 1. Additive manufacturing methods and modelling approaches: a critical review

*File: `Additive_manufacturing_methods_and_modelling_approaches_a_critical_rev__W1022778776.pdf`*

**Brief:** Maps additive manufacturing methods by process mechanism and reviews modelling approaches, aiming to find research gaps relevant to process control.

**Main notes:**

- AM is limited by low productivity, poor quality and uncertain mechanical properties
- Physical phenomena include melting, solidification, vaporization, heat and mass transfer
- Later sections review implications for closed-loop control

### 2. The Surface Energy Balance System (SEBS) for estimation of turbulent heat fluxes

*File: `The_Surface_Energy_Balance_System_SEBS_for_estimation_of_turbulent_hea__W2018318189.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes SEBS to estimate turbulent heat fluxes and evaporative fraction from satellite data plus meteorological information.

**Main notes:**

- Tools to derive albedo, emissivity, temperature and vegetation cover
- A model for roughness length for heat transfer
- New evaporative fraction formulation from energy balance at limiting cases
- Four data sets show accuracy comparable to in-situ measurement uncertainty
