### 1. A large-scale microelectromechanical-systems-based silicon photonics LiDAR

*File: `A_large_scale_microelectromechanical_systems_based_silicon_photonics_L__W4220706180.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reports a solid-state LiDAR with a 16,384-pixel MEMS-actuated focal plane switch array, overcoming the 512-pixel limit of earlier focal plane arrays.

**Main notes:**

- 70 x 70 degree field of view, 0.6 degree addressing resolution, beam divergence about 0.05 degrees
- 128 x 128 grating antennas and MEMS optical switches integrated monolithically on a 10 x 11 mm chip; 128 x 96 subarray tested
- FMCW ranging achieved 1.7 cm distance resolution
- Can be mass-produced in CMOS foundries

### 2. MEMS-tunable dielectric metasurface lens

*File: `MEMS_tunable_dielectric_metasurface_lens__W2886728826.pdf`*

**Brief:** Demonstrates tunable metasurface doublets driven by MEMS, replacing bulky varifocal lenses with thin, fast elements.

**Main notes:**

- More than 60 diopters (about 4%) optical power change for 1 micrometre movement
- Scanning frequency could reach a few kHz
- Combined with a third metasurface gives a roughly 1 mm thick microscope with about 500 micrometre or 40 degree corrected field and fast axial scanning
