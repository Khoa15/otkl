### 1. A State-of-the-art Review on the Intelligent Tool Holders in Machining

*File: `A_State_of_the_art_Review_on_the_Intelligent_Tool_Holders_in_Machining__W4390863233.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reviews intelligent tool holders that sense cutting signals such as force, vibration and temperature to regulate cutting and improve quality.

**Main notes:**

- Cutting force and heat are key factors in quality
- Signals include force, vibration and temperature
- Good signal data sets cut time, resource and labor costs

### 2. A review of artificial intelligence application for machining surface quality prediction

*File: `A_review_of_artificial_intelligence_application_for_machining_surface__W4407304724.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reviews machine and deep learning methods for predicting surface quality in CNC machining.

**Main notes:**

- Factors grouped into tool center positioning errors and tool edge-workpiece interaction
- Factors tabulated from research of the last five years
- Examines how AI models incorporate these factors, including transfer learning
