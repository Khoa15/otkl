### 1. A renewably sourced, circular photopolymer resin for additive manufacturing

*File: `A_renewably_sourced_circular_photopolymer_resin_for_additive_manufactu__W4396918932.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents a vat photopolymerization resin derived from renewable lipoates that can be depolymerized and reused in a closed loop.

**Main notes:**

- Existing resins mostly petroleum-based and not directly recyclable
- Platform built entirely from renewable lipoates
- Aims at circular, closed-loop printing

### 2. Pore evolution mechanisms during directed energy deposition additive manufacturing

*File: `Pore_evolution_mechanisms_during_directed_energy_deposition_additive_m__W4392129291.pdf`*

**Brief:** Uses in situ X-ray imaging and multi-physics modelling to reveal how pores form and evolve in DED.

**Main notes:**

- Gas-atomised powder bubbles enter the melt pool and migrate
- Small bubbles escape, coalesce or are entrapped by solidification fronts
- Marangoni flow overcomes buoyancy keeping large bubbles in the pool
- Large bubbles escape or are trapped once critical size is reached
