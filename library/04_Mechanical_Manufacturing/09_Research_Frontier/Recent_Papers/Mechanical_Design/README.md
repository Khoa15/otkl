### 1. Chemo-mechanical failure mechanisms of the silicon anode in solid-state batteries

*File: `Chemo_mechanical_failure_mechanisms_of_the_silicon_anode_in_solid_stat__W4391262793.pdf`*

**Brief:** Explains poor performance of silicon anodes in solid-state batteries.

**Main notes:**

- SEI growth at Si|Li6PS5Cl interface raises resistance in composite anodes
- Electrolyte-free Si anodes have enough conductivity and high capacity
- Void formation on delithiation causes larger interface stress there

### 2. Optimizing scaffold pore size for tissue engineering: insights across various tissue types

*File: `Optimizing_scaffold_pore_size_for_tissue_engineering_insights_across_v__W4404292906.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of how scaffold porosity and pore size affect tissue formation.

**Main notes:**

- Skin: ~1-2 um aids epidermal attachment; ~2-12 um dermal migration; ~40-100 um vascular structures
- Bone: multi-layered scaffolds with 50-100 um smaller pores (text cut off)
- Covers skin, bone, cardiovascular and lung tissue
