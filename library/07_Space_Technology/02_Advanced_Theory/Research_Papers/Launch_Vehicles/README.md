### 1. Mars Science Laboratory Mission and Science Investigation

*File: `Mars_Science_Laboratory_Mission_and_Science_Investigation__W2087153461.pdf`*

**Brief:** Describes the MSL mission to explore Mars habitability at Gale crater with the Curiosity rover, written before the August 2012 landing.

**Main notes:**

- Covers modern and ancient environments recorded in Gale crater stratigraphy.
- Curiosity is designed for at least one Mars year (about 23 months) and at least 20 km of driving.
- Paper also presents the science payload.

### 2. The Solar Probe Plus Mission: Humanity's First Visit to Our Star

*File: `The_Solar_Probe_Plus_Mission_Humanity_s_First_Visit_to_Our_Star__W2100723408.pdf`*

**Brief:** Describes Solar Probe Plus, the first spacecraft to fly into the low solar corona.

**Main notes:**

- Goals: coronal magnetic field structure and dynamics, heating and acceleration of corona and solar wind, and energetic particle acceleration.
- These questions have been a top priority for over five decades.
- The paper covers mission design and enabling technology and engineering developments.
