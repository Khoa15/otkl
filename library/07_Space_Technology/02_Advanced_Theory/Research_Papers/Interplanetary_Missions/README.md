### 1. Magnetospheric Multiscale Overview and Science Objectives

*File: `Magnetospheric_Multiscale_Overview_and_Science_Objectives__W2201603632.pdf`*

**Brief:** Overview of NASA's four-spacecraft MMS constellation, launched March 12, 2015, to study magnetic reconnection at Earth's magnetopause and magnetotail.

**Main notes:**

- Main goal is a definitive experiment on what causes field lines to reconnect in a collisionless plasma.
- Reconnection also occurs in interplanetary space and the solar corona, driving flares and coronal mass ejections.
- Relevant to laboratory and fusion research as well.

### 2. The Cluster Magnetic Field Investigation: overview of in-flight performance and initial results

*File: `The_Cluster_Magnetic_Field_Investigation_overview_of_in_flight_perform__W2139413516.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reports in-flight performance and first results of the magnetometers on the four Cluster spacecraft.

**Main notes:**

- Accurate magnetic field measurement along all four orbits is a primary mission objective.
- Four-point measurements allow 3D study of space plasma phenomena on scales comparable to the constellation size.
- They help separate temporal from spatial variations.
