### 2. Trajectory Design for the ESA LISA Mission

*File: `Trajectory_Design_for_the_ESA_LISA_Mission__W3119339632.pdf`*

**Brief:** Designs the transfer and science orbits for three LISA spacecraft in a triangular formation, moved by solar electric propulsion.

**Main notes:**

- Transfer and science orbits are optimized together.
- At 90 mN thrust, 1092 m/s per spacecraft suffices for all-year launch in 2034.
- Each launch month has a dedicated science orbit with corner angle variation of 60 deg +/-1.0 deg and arm length rate up to 10 m/s.
- Range/Doppler measurements plus early correction manoeuvres reduce insertion dispersions.
