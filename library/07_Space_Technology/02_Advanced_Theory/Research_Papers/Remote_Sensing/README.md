### 1. An assessment of the global impact of 21st century land use change on soil erosion

*File: `An_assessment_of_the_global_impact_of_21st_century_land_use_change_on__W2772366318.pdf`*

**Brief:** Builds a 250 m global potential soil erosion model from remote sensing, GIS modelling and census data, and assesses land use change effects.

**Main notes:**

- Estimates 35.9 Pg per year of soil eroded in 2012, at least two times lower than earlier reference values.
- Assesses land use change between 2001 and 2012 and the potential offset from conservation practices.
- Finds a potential overall increase in erosion driven by cropland expansion.
