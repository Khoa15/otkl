### 1. Science Objectives and Rationale for the Radiation Belt Storm Probes Mission

*File: `Science_Objectives_and_Rationale_for_the_Radiation_Belt_Storm_Probes_M__W2143422071.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents the NASA RBSP mission, which studies how high-energy particle populations in Earth's radiation belts are created, vary and evolve.

**Main notes:**

- Two spacecraft in similar highly elliptical, low-inclination orbits (1.1 x 5.8 RE, 10 deg).
- One spacecraft laps the other about every 2.5 months, separating spatial from temporal effects.
- Identical instrument suites measure particles and fields.
