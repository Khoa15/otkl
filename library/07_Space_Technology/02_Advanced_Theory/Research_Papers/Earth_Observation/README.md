### 1. A review of measurement-based assessments of the aerosol direct radiative effect and forcing

*File: `A_review_of_measurement_based_assessments_of_the_aerosol_direct_radiat__W1993065427.pdf`*

**Brief:** A multi-author review (Atmos. Chem. Phys., 2006) of how satellite and ground measurements are used to assess the aerosol direct radiative effect and forcing.

**Main notes:**

- Published in Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics 6, 613-666, 2006.
- Authors come from NASA, NOAA, universities and European labs.
- Focus is on measurement-based, rather than purely model-based, estimates.

### 2. The 30 m annual land cover dataset and its dynamics in China from 1990 to 2019

*File: `The_30_m_annual_land_cover_dataset_and_its_dynamics_in_China_from_1990__W3188524028.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents a 30 m annual land cover dataset for China built from observational imagery, to fill the lack of fine-resolution sequential land cover data there.

**Main notes:**

- Land cover affects energy exchange and the water and carbon cycles.
- China's land cover has changed strongly with economic development.
- Dataset covers 1990 to 2019 (Earth Syst. Sci. Data, 2021).
