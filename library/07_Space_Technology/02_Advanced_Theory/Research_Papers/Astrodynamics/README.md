### 1. How Many Impulses Redux

*File: `How_Many_Impulses_Redux__W2995438409.pdf`*

**Brief:** Revisits Edelbaum's 1967 question of how many velocity impulses minimize total impulse in an orbit transfer. Builds a minimum-fuel switching surface from the bottom up to answer it for multiple-revolution maneuvers.

**Main notes:**

- Sweeps time profiles of the minimum-fuel switching function for increasing thrust magnitude, up to the high-thrust limit.
- The continuous-thrust switching surface reveals the N-impulse solution.
- A fundamental minimum-thrust solution is pivotal for finding the optimal minimum-fuel maneuver at all thrust levels.
- The answer is generally not unique but is often a set of equal-delta-v extremals.

### 2. State-dependent trust region for successive convex programming for autonomous spacecraft

*File: `State_dependent_trust_region_for_successive_convex_programming_for_aut__W4395671310.pdf`*

**Brief:** Addresses on-board spacecraft trajectory optimization, where direct and indirect methods lack guaranteed convergence or need heavy computation. Proposes a state-dependent trust region within successive convex programming (SCVX).

**Main notes:**

- Heuristic control laws are cheap but sub-optimal.
- SCVX extends convex optimization to non-linear optimal control problems.
- The paper focuses on the trust-region design to support autonomous operation.
- Authors are from the Surrey Space Centre and the University of Auckland.
