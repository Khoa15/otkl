### 2. Unifying machine learning and quantum chemistry with a deep neural network for molecular wavefunctions

*File: `Unifying_machine_learning_and_quantum_chemistry_with_a_deep_neural_net__W2984234582.pdf`*

**Brief:** Presents a deep learning framework that predicts the quantum mechanical wavefunction in a local atomic-orbital basis, from which ground-state properties follow. Not about orbital mechanics despite its folder.

**Main notes:**

- Keeps access to electronic structure at force-field-like efficiency.
- Representation is analytically differentiable.
- Examples suggest inverse design of molecules for target electronic properties.
