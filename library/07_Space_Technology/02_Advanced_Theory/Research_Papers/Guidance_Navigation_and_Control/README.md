### 1. Characterizing Satellite Geometry via Accelerated 3D Gaussian Splatting

*File: `Characterizing_Satellite_Geometry_via_Accelerated_3D_Gaussian_Splattin__W4392163669.pdf`*

**Brief:** Targets autonomous on-orbit servicing, inspection and debris removal, which need the geometry of non-cooperative targets. Proposes mapping satellite geometry with 3D Gaussian Splatting that can run on current spaceflight hardware.

**Main notes:**

- Rendezvous and proximity operations need robust target characterization.
- Autonomy is needed because of safety concerns and ground-control lag.
- The approach is designed for flight-available computing resources.

### 2. Multidisciplinary design modeling and optimization for satellite with maneuver capability

*File: `Multidisciplinary_design_modeling_and_optimizationfor_satellite_with_m__W1984501571.pdf`*

**Brief:** Applies collaborative optimization to a multidisciplinary design model for a satellite with maneuver capability.

**Main notes:**

- Possessed and needed delta-v measure maneuvering capability and are combined with total mass as the system-level objective.
- Design variables cover GNC, power and structure subsystems.
- Engineering analysis models were built for each discipline and a program system was developed to solve the problem.
