### 1. Long-Term Sorption of Metals Is Similar among Plastic Types

*File: `Long_Term_Sorption_of_Metals_Is_Similar_among_Plastic_Types_Implicatio__W2017388749.pdf`*

**Brief:** Studies metal accumulation on plastic debris in seawater. Concerns aquatic plastic debris, not orbital debris.

**Main notes:**

- Five plastic types: PET, HDPE, PVC, LDPE and PP.
- Nine metals measured at three San Diego Bay sites at 1, 3, 6, 9 and 12 months.
- Accumulation patterns were not consistent over space and time.

### 2. Phagocytosis of Microglia in the Central Nervous System Diseases

*File: `Phagocytosis_of_Microglia_in_the_Central_Nervous_System_Diseases__W2063995449.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of microglial phagocytosis in neurological diseases. Biomedical topic, not space debris.

**Main notes:**

- Activated microglia become motile, secrete cytokines and phagocytose debris or damaged neurons.
- It is unclear whether phagocytosis is beneficial or detrimental to repair.
- Covers Alzheimer's, multiple sclerosis, Parkinson's, brain injury and ischemia.
