### 1. Global-scale magnetosphere convection driven by dayside magnetic reconnection

*File: `Global_scale_magnetosphere_convection_driven_by_dayside_magnetic_recon__W4391047256.pdf`*

**Brief:** Provides direct evidence that magnetospheric convection can be driven solely by dayside reconnection.

**Main notes:**

- Tied to evolution of Region 1 and Region 2 field-aligned currents.
- Global simulations show intensified convection progressing dayside to nightside within 10-20 minutes after a southward turning.
- Challenges reliance on nightside tail reconnection in the Dungey cycle.

### 2. How Can Optical Communications Shape the Future of Deep Space Communications? A Survey

*File: `How_Can_Optical_Communications_Shape_the_Future_of_Deep_Space_Communic__W4398187781.pdf`*

**Brief:** Survey of deep space optical communications (free space optical) as an alternative to RF.

**Main notes:**

- FSO offers lower size, weight and power and higher capacity than RF.
- Discusses pros and cons plus physical and networking characteristics.
- Claims the first comprehensive deep-space communications survey in a decade.
