### 1. Highly agile flat swimming robot

*File: `Highly_agile_flat_swimming_robot__W4407736517.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reports a centimeter-scale untethered swimming robot with soft undulating pectoral fins. Not about space debris.

**Main notes:**

- Speeds of 5.1 cm/s translation and 195 deg/s rotation.
- Fins are millimeter-thin with electrically excited traveling waves.
- Navigates narrow spaces on cluttered water surfaces.

### 2. Imaging shapes of atomic nuclei in high-energy nuclear collisions

*File: `Imaging_shapes_of_atomic_nuclei_in_high_energy_nuclear_collisions__W4404105265.pdf`*

**Brief:** STAR Collaboration paper introducing collective-flow-assisted nuclear shape imaging. Nuclear physics, not space debris.

**Main notes:**

- Collides nuclei at ultrarelativistic speeds and analyses the collective response of outgoing debris.
- Benchmarked on uranium-238.
- Finds a large deformation.
