### 2. Global critical soil moisture thresholds of plant water stress

*File: `Global_critical_soil_moisture_thresholds_of_plant_water_stress__W4399382258.pdf`*

**Brief:** Maps the critical soil moisture threshold at which dry-downs begin to limit evapotranspiration.

**Main notes:**

- Combines satellite diurnal land surface temperature amplitude and soil moisture during dry-downs, checked with flux tower data.
- Average global threshold is 0.19 m3/m3.
- Useful for climate, water resource and food production projections.
