### 1. Distributed computing for physics-based data-driven reduced modeling at scale: rotating detonation rocket engine

*File: `Distributed_computing_for_physics_based_data_driven_reduced_modeling_a__W4409417239.pdf`*

**Brief:** Addresses the cost of rotating detonation rocket engine simulations, which take millions of core hours, using data-driven reduced-order models built with distributed computing.

**Main notes:**

- ROMs aim to be cheap yet accurate approximations.
- Enables design exploration and risk assessment.

### 2. Selection of propulsion characteristics for systematic assessment of future European RLV options

*File: `Selection_of_propulsion_characteristics_for_systematic_assessment_of_f__W4402632154.pdf`*

**Brief:** Compares liquid propellant combinations and defines main-stage engines for a study on future European reusable launchers.

**Main notes:**

- Fuels with LOX: LH2, methane, propane and RP1.
- Cycle options: open gas-generator and closed staged-combustion.
- Eight generic engines with nozzle expansion sub-variants.
