### 1. Genuine quantum scars in many-body spin systems

*File: `Genuine_quantum_scars_in_many_body_spin_systems__W4403007016.pdf`*

**Brief:** Shows that exponentially many eigenstates of many-body spin systems are scarred along unstable classical periodic orbits.

**Main notes:**

- Scarring weakly breaks ergodicity while eigenstates remain thermal.
- Eigenstate thermalization hypothesis is still fulfilled.
- Demonstrated on a large family of spin models.

### 2. Post-Newtonian theory for gravitational waves

*File: `Post_Newtonian_theory_for_gravitational_waves__W4400493602.pdf`*

**Brief:** Review of the MPM-PN formalism for modelling inspiralling compact binaries and their gravitational waves.

**Main notes:**

- Needed for LIGO, Virgo, KAGRA and future detectors including LISA.
- Merges multipolar-post-Minkowskian and post-Newtonian expansions.
- Gives equations of motion up to 4PN.
