### 1. Aided Kalman Filter Models for GNSS-based Space Navigation

*File: `Aided_Kalman_Filter_Models_for_GNSS_Based_Space_Navigation__W4398187679.pdf`*

**Brief:** Proposes a Trajectory-Aware Extended Kalman Filter for GNSS-based orbit determination at high altitudes, where signal availability and geometry are poor.

**Main notes:**

- Uses a pre-mission planned trajectory as aiding data.
- Aims at precise OD relying only on GNSS measurements.
- Two TA-EKF designs: observation-domain and state-domain integration.

### 2. Trajectory Stability and Signature Diagnostics for Comet-Based Interstellar Navigation

*File: `Trajectory_Stability_and_Signature_Diagnostics_for_Comet_Based_Interst__W7138895340.pdf`*

**Brief:** Theoretical study (March 2026) of stability requirements if volatile-rich comet-like interstellar objects were used for deep-space navigation with jet-actuated correction.

**Main notes:**

- Develops a stability framework for trajectory tracking.
- Tracking residuals modeled as disturbances balanced by corrective action.
- Derives stability conditions at several levels, including disturbance-energy and outer-loop contraction.
