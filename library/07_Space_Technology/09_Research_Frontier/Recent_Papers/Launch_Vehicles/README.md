### 1. AI Algorithm for Predicting and Optimizing Trajectory of UAV Swarm

*File: `AI_algorithm_for_predicting_and_optimizing_trajectory_of_massive_UAV_s__W4406109238.pdf`*

**Brief:** Applies AI to generating UAV fleet trajectories, addressing path prediction and collision avoidance.

**Main notes:**

- Tests various activation functions in a single-hidden-layer feedforward network, improving accuracy over prior work.
- Introduces AdaptoSwelliGauss, combining Swish, Elliott and a scaled, shifted Gaussian.

### 2. Overview of system study on recovery methods for reusable first stages of future European launchers

*File: `Overview_of_system_study_on_recovery_methods_for_reusable_first_stages__W4400187169.pdf`*

**Brief:** Reviews recovery methods for reusable launch vehicle first stages and how each choice drives system design (CEAS Space Journal, 2025).

**Main notes:**

- Economics favor recovering large parts such as entire stages.
- Recovered stages carry significant energy and mass.
- Different methods need different technologies and competencies.
